# Sarric — DM notes

Companion to [`sarric.md`](sarric.md) (Jeremy's sheet). Our design notes live here so the
sheet stays the player's. Moved out of the sheet 8/16.

---

## The camp

The camp is what Sarric ran from. He set a fire that killed people he had not meant to
kill, and he left. He has continued doing good work since, and the good work is real. He
has also never spoken about the camp to anyone.

His confession in the Hall of Two Truths will almost certainly be about the camp. He will
want to contextualize it — the lord, the village, the injustice that came before. Let him,
then have nobody answer him. Anubis weighs the fire and not the reasons for it.

The confession in its simplest form: *"I set a fire and I heard screaming and I didn't go
back."*

Whether he arrives at that simplicity on his own is up to Jeremy.

[Jasper](jasper-dm-notes.md) may not know about the camp. If he doesn't, the Hall is where
he finds out, in front of Osiris, with no chance to react privately.

## The wanted posters `[Will 8/16]`

Run the wanted status as a live thread rather than a piece of backstory. The poster shows
a cloaked figure with bees around him, and Sarric carries the hive everywhere, so anyone
who has looked at the poster will know him.

- **Put posters where the party already is.** A board outside a magistrate's office, a
  tavern wall, a Company ore-port. The party can see one without anyone recognizing him,
  and Sarric still has to decide whether to take it down.
- **Whoever hung it wants him for the fire, and the fire killed men the Company was paying
  to displace people.** The Company owns the magistrates
  ([the Company](../world/nila/lore/the-company.md)), so the magistrates holding his poster
  are the ones who license the mine at
  [Haals](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-haals-runoff.md).
- `[OPEN]` Who is actually still looking, and how hard. A standing bounty, a single
  surviving relative, or a filed paper nobody has read in years — pick before the party
  can investigate it.
- `[OPEN]` Whether anyone from the camp survived, and whether Jeremy wants that.

## The earth dreams

Sarric's people go back into the land when they die and come up out of it carrying the
memories of the lives that went in ([lineages & afterlives §5, §11](../world/nila/lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md)).
Jeremy was offered this and it was framed as ordinary — see
[player messages — earth dreams](../sessions/player-messages-earth-dreams.md). He dreamed an
ancient river bank in the tombs.

It comes back at
[Haals](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-haals-runoff.md): the mountain
the Company is cutting into is firbolg dead, and Sarric and [Pax](pax-dm-notes.md) dream
it harder the closer they get. Sarric has his people's word for what is happening and Pax
does not, so Sarric can name it and be casual about it while Pax reads it as the Green
Revelation.

## Campaign connections

- The organization connects to the campaign's land destruction and volcanism themes.
- His hatred of the wealthy who destroy land for profit puts him in conflict with Preem
  once that operation is understood, and with the
  [Company](../world/nila/lore/the-company.md) before that.
- His belief that the world feels pain when it is hurt holds at Haals, where the poisoned
  water comes off a mountain that is firbolg dead.
- Describe the hive in any scene he is in.
