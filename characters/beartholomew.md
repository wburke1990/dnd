# Beartholomew — Aniess's friend from the cohort `[draft / new — Will 8/25]`

> **DM reference.** The bear-infused Echo Knight from
> [Aniess](aniess.md)'s training cohort in the **Order of the Emplere Cnihtas**.
> Greg named him and set the friendship; everything below is DM material built on
> that line. He is the NPC who makes the ask in
> [the godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md).
> Legend: `[settling]` · `[loose]` · `[OPEN]`.

**Race:** human, merged with a bear
**Class:** Fighter (Echo Knight)
**Affiliation:** the Order of the Emplere Cnihtas — the same covert arm as Aniess
**Where he is:** [Aar](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/lore/aar.md), on the Company's
books as an ore guard

---

## Who he is `[settling]`

Seven feet, and wide. Brown fur over most of him, a bear's head, a man's hands. He
wears a Company guard's coat that does not fit him. He speaks slowly and finishes his
sentences.

He came up with Aniess. They were taken off the street in the same year, trained
in the same room, and put through the rite in the same season — she with a snake,
he with a bear. He carried her back to the compound after the rite when she could
not walk. He has not asked her for anything since.

The Order trained him to pass unseen, then stopped sending him through doors and
started placing him where they wanted a big man standing in the open.

## The posting `[settling]`

He has been an **ore guard at Aar for four months**, hired off the wharf like any
other big man, and the Order put him there. He stands on the ore, watches what comes
off the canal and what goes onto the sea barges, and reports it. Nobody at the port
knows him as anything else.

**He cannot leave the post.** If he walks off the wharf for a week to go up the canal,
he loses the job and the Order loses the placement. He will not be seen near a theft in
the town he is paid to guard either: if the party are caught, he does not know them.

## The echo `[settling]`

He can put a copy of himself across a room, over a wall, or on the far side of a
shed door. The copy moves, watches, and takes a blow meant for
him. It cannot lift a crate, turn a key, or carry anything out. It is how he watches the guest house on the
basin without leaving his post.

## How to play him `[settling]`

He asks for what he wants first. He calls Aniess by her name rather than her
designation.

He talks about the men he trained with and the winter the compound ran out of fuel.
Asked what the Order is for, he says *"to bring the lost brethren home."*

## What he wants `[settling]`

The **peseshkef** on Setha Renk's belt in the guest house
([the godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md)),
carried up the canal to his man at Copaa. He has seen it in the port. He cannot leave
his post to take it and he cannot carry it upriver, and Renk's boat north comes in a
few days.
