# Beartholomew — Aniess's friend from the cohort `[draft / new — Will 8/25]`

> **DM reference.** The bear-infused Echo Knight from
> [Aniess](aniess.md)'s training cohort in the **Order of the Emplere Cnihtas**.
> Greg named him and set the friendship; everything below is DM material built on
> that line. He is the NPC who makes the ask in
> [the godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md).
> Legend: `[settling]` · `[loose]` · `[OPEN]`.

**Race:** human, merged with a bear
**Class:** Fighter (Echo Knight)
**Affiliation:** the Order of the Emplere Cnihtas — the same covert arm as Aniess
**Where he is:** [Aar](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/lore/aar.md), the Company ore-port

---

## Who he is `[settling]`

Seven feet, and wide. Brown fur over most of him, a bear's head, a man's hands.
He wears Company-cut dock clothes that do not fit him and does not pretend they
do. He speaks slowly and finishes his sentences.

He came up with Aniess. They were taken off the street in the same year, trained
in the same room, and put through the rite in the same season — she with a snake,
he with a bear. He carried her back to the compound after the rite when she could
not walk. He has not asked her for anything since.

The Order trained him to pass unseen. He is seven feet tall with a bear's head. He
knows it. He does the fighting and the carrying.

## The echo `[settling]`

He can put a copy of himself across a room, over a wall, or on the far side of a
shed door. The copy moves, watches, and takes a blow meant for
him. It cannot lift a crate, turn a key, or carry anything out. He has looked into the Company
sheds at Aar with the copy and cannot touch what is in them.

## How to play him `[settling]`

Warm and direct. He asks for what he wants first and does not work up to it. He
calls Aniess by her name. No one else does.

He talks about the men he trained with, the food, the winter the compound ran out of fuel.
Asked what the Order is for, he says what he was told.

## What he wants `[settling]`

The knife in the Company's outbound crate at Aar
([the godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md)).
He was posted here to find it and put it on a ship for Kuru. He has found it. He
cannot get it. The barge sails at first light.
