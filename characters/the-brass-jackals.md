# The Brass Jackals — Preem's crew out of Kuru

> **NPC crew.** Five freelancers from the north, hired by [Preem](preem.md) to
> hunt names and paid badly for it. They are broke, a member or two down from
> where they started, and stuck in port without a boat. The party first crossed
> them on Preem's stairs in [session one](../sessions/session-01.md) and drank
> with them at the Tally House in Aar in
> [session 13](../sessions/session-13.md).
>
> **This sheet is the reference.** The scene material — appearance, hook lines,
> the full backstory of each member, the DM-only paragraphs — is in
> [the rival crews](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#the-brass-jackals--the-ones-it-costs).
> This file is who they are in one page, plus everything that has passed between
> them and the party.

## Snapshot

- `[settled]` **What they are.** Foreign freelancers from Kuru. Before Preem they
  were scavengers of the Muspel reconquest, trailing the holy war into Musleheim
  to strip battlefields and pick over ruined ports. They now rob tombs.
- `[settled]` **Their dead.** Their founder, **Old Brakker**, went down with a
  salvage that flooded, which is how Veska came to lead a crew she did not found.
  The most recent name in Hadda's book is **Riska**, their young scout, lost on the
  road not long ago. Sefi took the scout's job and is not ready.
- `[settled]` **What they are for at the table.** They are not paired with the PCs
  the way the [Lapis Writ](the-lapis-writ.md) are. They rob graves because the
  alternative is starving. Loud and sneering in a group; alone, Hadda talks about
  the pay being too high, Sefi about the dark, and Pim about his bottles.
- `[settled]` **They are at the bottom of Preem's standings** and may drop out of
  them as the [Ashfoot](the-ashfoot.md) did
  ([rival crew plots](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/rival-crew-plots.md)).
- `[settled]` **They have no healer.** Veska has prepared no spells since Sukkek
  and does not explain it to them.
- 🔒 `[settled]` **Preem does not trust them and has a spy inside.** Pim's
  centipede is a quasit and it is Preem's ([Preem's demons](preems-demons.md)).

## The five

**[Veska Tull](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#veska-tull--human--brass-jackals-leader-doruks-cross-crew-mirror)**
— human, leader, Cleric 3 (Light), lapsed.

A poor Kuru girl with a talent for preaching who rose fast in the Church of Sunne,
preaching the reconquest as holy work. Her job was the pogroms: she went ahead of
the torch-men and worked a port's faithful into fury before the burning. It ended
at **Sukkek**, where she walked into the burned quarter at dawn to bless the ground
and found town-born half-orc families, old people and children. She stole a
rowboat and rowed to Raand alone. 🔒 The Church believes she died in that fire and
she lets them. If she ever preaches against them, Kuru learns she survived Sukkek.
She makes one cheap drink last all evening.

**[Hadda Vimm](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#hadda-vimm--dwarf--brass-jackals-quartermaster)**
— dwarf, quartermaster, Rogue 3 (Thief), Expertise in Investigation and Insight.

She keeps the coin, the supplies, the map, and the record of every Jackal they have
buried, by name, going back to the scavenging years. She prices
everyone's gear out of habit. The pay on this job is too high for grave-robbing,
Preem runs an odd number of crews at once, and the sites are a long way to send
people. She suspects it before she has proof. 🔒 She has started a second tally she
shows nobody, listing what she cannot explain about this job. They are broke and
there is no other work.

**[Sefi](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#sefi--halfling--brass-jackals-scout)**
— lightfoot halfling, scout, Rogue 2.

A war-orphan the Jackals pulled out of a port wrecked in the reconquest, raised as
the crew's kid. She worshipped Riska and pulled on the kit when Riska did not come
back. She goes in first — down the shaft or the stairs, whatever comes first — and
says so to anyone who will listen. She is the lowest level of anyone in either crew
and a level below the scout she replaced. Hadda waters her drink. 🔒 She only
half-knows that Riska died doing this job, and Veska cannot bear to lose her.

**[Mogg](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#mogg--half-orc--brass-jackals-enforcer--storyteller-doruks-cross-crew-mirror)**
— half-orc, enforcer and storyteller, Barbarian 3 (Path of the Ancestral
Guardian).

Every half-orc descends from the drowned Muspel, and Mogg tells the old sagas —
Hjörungavágr, the king who would not strike his sail — because nobody else who knew
them is alive. *A tale for a cup* is the standing deal.
He does the crew's violent work and says afterwards that it stays with him. When he rages
the spirits of the dead Muspel come up around him. His kin died in the burnings the
Writ's Doruk lit and Veska preached, and none of the three knows what the other two
did.

> `[verbatim — do not restyle]` Mogg's sagas are built out of real public-domain
> saga text and are off limits to the style pass and to both critics. They live in
> [the rival crews → The opening](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md)
> and [ships and sea power](../world/the-sea/lore/ships-and-sea-power.md).

**[Pim Riddle](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md#pim-riddle--gnome--brass-jackals-alchemist--trap-breaker)**
— gnome, alchemist and trap-breaker, Artificer 3 (Alchemist).

A real alchemist once, who landed with the Jackals because grave-work pays and
nothing else would take a nervous gnome. The work means going down first, alone,
with his hands on the traps, so he mixes compounds to get through it: one
to get down the shaft, one so his hands do not shake on a trigger-stone, one for
the dreams after. 🔒 He knows what the bottles are doing to him and takes the next
dose anyway. A long dark centipede he calls his lab familiar rides in a
pocket and wanders off on its own.

🔒 **The centipede is Preem's quasit,** planted the way the bat was planted on
Jasper and the toad on Calix. It murmurs to Pim, and he is confused enough by his
own compounds that he takes the voice for more of the same. That is why Preem
picked him. It answers to Preem alone, and what Preem wants from it is early word
on Hadda's suspicions.

## Numbers

All five were statted `[new — Will 9/1]`. The class lines above are the whole of
it; hit points and AC have not been written down the way the
[Writ's](the-lapis-writ.md) were. Three things come up in play:

- **No healer.** Veska prepares nothing.
- **Mogg's Ancestral Guardians** raise the dead Muspel around him, which is what
  happens if the fight with Doruk goes past fists. Half-orc Relentless Endurance
  drops him to 1 hit point instead of 0, once per long rest.
- **Pim's compounds** are how the Jackals open a sealed or trapped door.

## How they travel

They own a cheap, rotting dwarf-built tub and nobody aboard can sail or mend it
competently. They travel on their own, arrive after everyone else, and a storm
could sink the boat. At Aar they priced the Writ's berth north at a year of their income
and watched somebody find the Writ one in a day. Veska says they will walk the four
hundred miles if they have to.

`[loose]` **Fixing their boat.** Their hull is rotting and
[Blackacre](blackacre.md) can fix a wooden boat without a shipyard. Fixing it keeps
them working for Preem.

## What has happened with the party

`[played]` **Session 1 — the five on the stairs.** The party's audience with Preem
ended when a big centipede came up the stairs with five people behind it, and the
party were bumped out of the office. **The party clocked the centipede.**
`[settled, retroactive]` That crew of five was the Brass Jackals, and the centipede
is Preem's — each crew is led up those stairs by its own familiar, and the
centipede carried the Jackals' summons the way the bat carried the party's
([session 1](../sessions/session-01.md)).

`[played]` **Session 13 — the evening at the Tally House, Aar.** Both crews and the
party in one room. What happened:

- **The opening.** Mogg was up on a bench mid-saga and the room was listening, and
  Doruk was drunk and huffing at it from the Writ's end. Nobody started anything.
- **Pax and Pim got on.** They compared hearing voices, and **Pax gave him a
  mushroom** — one of the large purple-spotted, orange-gilled ones from the market.
  Pax handed a hallucinogen to a man already taking four compounds a day who cannot
  go down a hole sober. **Pax met the centipede** and does not know what it is.
- **Sarric produced bees.**
- **Pim raised the Sacred Hunt** and asked whether the party would be hunters or
  hunted. `[settled at the table 9/1]` He told them **a hunter's place costs
  5,000 gp.** The party do not have it and said so
  ([the game preserve](../world/suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/the-game-preserve.md)).
- **Pax introduced Pim to Sarric.**
- **Sefi talked back to Setha.** When Renk called the Jackals *"those amateurs?"*
  across the room, Sefi answered her: *"Setha! Don't be such a jerk!"* Nobody else
  in either crew talks to Renk that way, and Renk had nothing to say to it.
- **The brawl did not start.** Doruk and Mogg were both drunk in the same room all
  evening and nobody told either of them what the other is
  ([the fight](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-run-to-copaa.md#the-fight--doruk-and-mogg-run)).

`[played]` **Session 15 — the party sailed and the Jackals did not.** The party left
Aar by sea. The Jackals are broke, have no berth, and are still there.

## What each side knows

- **The party know** the Jackals are name-hunters too, that they are broke and
  waiting on a boat, that Pim hears something and takes bottles for it, and that a
  hunter's seat at the Hunt costs 5,000 gp.
- **The party do not know** that the Jackals are Preem's, that these are the five
  who came up the stairs in session one, or that the centipede Pax met is the one
  they clocked that day.
- **The Jackals have not asked the party who they work for** and nobody has told
  them. Unlike the Writ, the two crews have not identified each other.
- 🔒 **Preem knows whatever the centipede saw at the Tally House.**

## Hooks

1. **Sefi.** Help the kid and the Jackals will be grateful. Leave her and she keeps
   going down shafts she is not ready for. Veska wants to keep her alive.
2. **Pim's mushroom.** Pax handed him something. Nothing has come of it yet.
3. **The boat.** Fix it, buy them passage, or leave them on the beach.
4. **Work out what the centipede is.** Same thread as Calix's toad. Free it or kill
   it and Preem loses his spy on the crew he does not trust.
5. **Veska's name.** Kuru's records list her as dead. Anyone who learns otherwise
   can give her to the Church.
6. **Doruk, Mogg and Veska.** They drink twenty feet apart and none of the three
   knows the other two's part in the burnings.
