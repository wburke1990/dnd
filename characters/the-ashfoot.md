# The Ashfoot — the crew that has been out too long

> **NPC crew, not yet used.** Five of [Preem](preem.md)'s name-hunters who have
> run the [Valley of the Kings](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/valley-of-the-kings.md)
> and pushed on to the scattered sites. **The party have never met them.** They
> are written for a later city, far from Maalm, re-provisioning between distant
> tombs — the distance is why they are short of supplies and cannot resupply
> quickly.
>
> **This sheet is unfinished on purpose.** The crew is meant to be written out
> once the PCs are closer to them, member by member, and matched to the PCs as they
> are then. The hooks below are placeholders, not commitments.

## Snapshot

- `[settled]` **Do not use them in Maalm.** They only make sense weeks out among
  the scattered sites. A crew working those would not backtrack to the city.
- `[settled]` **What they are.** They have been out too long. Ragged, lean,
  counting heads, and starting to suspect the names do something, without knowing
  what.
- `[settled]` **Their rank.** The lowest of Preem's crews, below the
  [Lapis Writ](the-lapis-writ.md) and below the
  [Brass Jackals](the-brass-jackals.md)
  ([rival crew plots](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/rival-crew-plots.md)).
- `[settled]` **They cannot leave.** Their hull is failing, they have no one who
  can mend it, and the only ship that comes that far out is the occasional Mukka
  at rates they cannot pay.

## The five

| Name | Species | Role | What they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Korbo Sann** | half-orc | leader | Tired and watchful. Has buried crew and counts heads constantly. |
| **Nem the Pale** | human | tomb-guide | Knows the tombs better than anyone. Flinches at the word *names*. |
| **Bittel Roon** | halfling | thief | Twitchy, and pocketing names to sell on the side. |
| **Hessa Drum** | dwarf | lapsed priest | Convinced the crews are doing something damnable, and drinks to not think about it. |
| **Olen Vask** | elf | survivor | The only holdover from an earlier crew that did not come back. Quiet, and knows more about the tombs than the rest of them. |

**Olen was not theirs.** Korbo's people found him alone in the tombs, starving and
not speaking, the last of an earlier Preem crew that never reported back, and took
him on. He has done the Valley twice.

## What they saw

`[settled]` **The sentinel.** The tombs are five thousand years old and nothing
living is still down there. The one thing that survives with a true name is the
sentinel left at the threshold — a bound spirit, a desiccated watchman, or a lesser
demon. Nem read a name off the wall, or Bittel lifted the tablet it was carved on,
and the guardian **answered to it**: knelt and went slack.

They do not understand what they saw. They know a name can make a thing obey, and
they want to be far away before they have to think about it. Olen warns them.
Hessa confirms it when drunk.

## What has happened with the party

**Nothing.** The party have not met them and have not heard the name. `[OPEN]`

## Hooks

1. **Bittel's side trade.** He is selling names Preem paid for. The party could use
   it against Preem.
2. **Olen's warning.** He has done the Valley twice and watched a crew not come
   back. He is the first NPC who can tell the party what the names do, without
   knowing why.
3. **They cannot leave.** They have no way home. The party own a boat.
