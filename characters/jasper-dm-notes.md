## The alchemical sickness — his people, and the poison

Jasper's people were humanoids — humans, maybe — that the alchemical
sickness mutated after the last Ragnarok. The Earth Genasi are what the
sickness made of them. The rest of the party never went through that; their
magic is a different kind.

This is our reading of Doug's backstory, not a rewrite of it. Doug wrote the
environmental disaster that destroyed Jasper's community, the oligarchs, and
the Earth Genasi descent from humanoids. We tie that disaster to the world's
alchemical sickness.

Where it pays off:

- **The Hell-Tree.** The tree's poison is that same sickness (see
  [the-hell-tree.md](../encounters/the-hell-tree.md)). It does not mutate or
  harm Jasper. It makes him stronger — more so the closer he gets to the
  tree, more so again down the stair. The rest of the party mutates; he does
  not.

## The Confession

Jasper's confession in the Hall of Two Truths is not a conventional sin. It is survivor's guilt, stated plainly:

*"I lived. Everyone else didn't. I was fourteen. I have never understood why that happened and I have never stopped feeling that it should have been different. I don't know what I could have done. I don't know if that matters."*

Anubis looks at him for a long moment after he speaks. Then nods. The absence of judgment is its own kind of relief. His openness — the fact that he has nothing he wouldn't talk about — means the Hall does not find him in the same way it finds the others. He arrives honest and leaves the same way.

**On his black and white thinking:**

The dead man's story will be difficult for Jasper. A man who did something wrong out of love is harder for a blunt instrument to process than a straightforward oppressor. The dream quest does not resolve this for him. It just introduces the complication.

**On Sarric and the camp:**

Jasper may not know about the camp. If he doesn't, hearing Sarric's confession in the Hall of Two Truths is the moment he finds out — in front of Osiris, with no ability to react privately. What a rock person's face does when they hear that is up to Doug.

## His hometown — a Company coffee town in Musleheim `[settled — play, S10]`

Settled in play: Jasper's home town is a **[Company](../lore/the-company.md) coffee-plantation
town in Musleheim** — Jogo named the plantations in his hometown at the coffeehouse
([Session 10](../sessions/session-10.md)). That places his line in **Musleheim**, answering
the open question in [lineages & afterlives §7](../lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md) (earth
genasi are the Bad-Pass mutation of the Muspel folk).

- **The Company is the oligarch Doug wrote.** Doug's backstory has oligarchs and an
  environmental disaster wreck Jasper's community; the Company's plantation clearances in
  Musleheim — enclosing the orc herders' grazing land into coffee rows and driving them off
  ([the Company](../lore/the-company.md)) — give that a name and a trade. The empire whose
  coffee V. served is the one that cleared his home ground.
- **Same wound as Sarric's Eet.** The Company now sits on two PCs' homelands — Sarric's Eet
  and Jasper's Musleheim town — so V.'s "the coffee isn't clean" cup points at both.
- `[OPEN]` a name for the town; and whether his community's destruction is the recent Company
  clearance, the lingering Bad-Pass sickness (above), or both.
