## Settled

Brauron's founder came out of a fruit of [the hell-tree](../encounters/the-hell-tree.md).
Someone plucked the fruit, carried it out of the deadland, and gave it water when it
asked. Whoever carried it raised what came out, and people settled around them.

The town tells this as [Momotarō](../../../../handouts/momotaro.md) — the childless
couple, the great peach, the boy who steps out. A statue of the boy stands in the
square, with the text cut into its base, and the gnomes act the story in the street.

The founder was a newborn demon. For what one is, what it says, and the water it asks
for, see
[what comes out of the fruit](../encounters/the-hell-tree.md#what-comes-out-of-the-fruit).

## Its name `[OPEN]`

Whoever raised it either heard it say its own name or gave it one, and a given name
binds the same as the name it was born with. So a mortal has held the true name of
Brauron's founder since the town began.

Where that name is now is open — cut into the statue base, kept by the temple, or lost.
Preem buys names (see [the name quest](../../../nila/lore/demons-and-the-hotspot.md)).
The party comes into the town on his errand, and the founding text is on public
display.

## The cult `[OPEN]`

The priestesses serve Artemis as Hecate here, of the moon and the dead, and the deadland
and the tree are hers under that name
([the peach town](../encounters/the-peach-town.md)). Whether the cult came with the
founder or came after.

## The dragonborn founders `[OPEN]`

The wandering dragonborn founded nearly every settlement in the world
([wandering dragonborn](../../../the-sea/lore/wandering-dragonborn.md)), and Brauron's
gnomes are dragon-descended. Whether a wandering dragonborn carried the fruit out, or
whether Brauron is an exception.
