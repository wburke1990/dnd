## Summary

Brauron's founder, Momotarō, came out of a fruit of
[the hell-tree](../encounters/the-hell-tree.md). The fruit floated down the river out of
the deadland to an old gnome couple, who gave the child water when he asked and raised
him. Gnomes coming north settled around their house, and the town grew there.

A statue of Momotarō stands in the square. The passage of the
[Momotarō](../../../../handouts/momotaro.md) tale where the peach splits is cut into its
base, and the gnomes act the story in the street.

Momotarō was a newborn demon
([what comes out of the fruit](../encounters/the-hell-tree.md#what-comes-out-of-the-fruit)).

---

## The founder

**Who found him.** An old gnome couple with no children, living on the river that runs
out of the deadland south to the sea. This was about four thousand years ago, when
gnomes were leaving the túatha and going north up the coast past
[Raand](../../../nila/lore/nila-atlas.md)
([the fallen gnome kingdoms](../../raand-copaa/lore/the-fallen-gnome-kingdom.md)). A fruit
off the tree fell in the river and floated down to them. The old woman was washing
clothes and pulled it out. The old man went to cut it, and it split, and a child stepped
out and asked for water. They gave him water.

**His name.** They named him Momotarō.

**What he built.**

- **The town.** Gnomes going north stopped at the old couple's house on the river.
  Within a generation there was a town, and Momotarō was running it.
- **The grove.** He planted the peach trees that ring the town.
- **The sanctuary.** He dedicated the deadland and the tree to the gnomes' goddess (see
  [the cult](#the-cult)).

---

## The cult

**The goddess came with the gnomes, before the town.** The Kalikhat refugees who took
Suartleheim Eet brought her up the coast with them (see
[the fallen gnome kingdoms](../../raand-copaa/lore/the-fallen-gnome-kingdom.md)), and
Brauron is named for her sanctuary, where girls serve her as bears. The
bee-priestesses are older than Brauron.

**Momotarō dedicated the deadland to her.** The gnomes worshipped her as goddess of the
hunt and of girls. He dedicated the deadland and the tree to her as Hecate, goddess of
the moon and the dead, and the priestesses turn back anyone who tries to enter the
deadland.

**The bee-priestesses (the Melissae)** run the hunt and keep the roll of the quarry. When a
priestess writes a quarry's name on the roll, she asks first where to send the
quarry's pay
([the dressing](../encounters/dressing-the-stag.md)). The sacred hunt runs once a year in
[the game preserve](../encounters/the-game-preserve.md).

**The eunuch priests (the Megabyzoi)** keep the
[Attis](../../../../handouts/the-birth-of-attis.md) mystery. At every dressing they stand
along the wall and watch.

## In play

The party comes to the town first and the tree last.

**In town, before the hunt.** They watch the play, read the statue base, see the
fountain, and walk through the grove.

`[Aniess]` **Timebiter** on a splinter of the oldest tree in the grove returns the
grove's age. It is the town's age, to the decade.

**Day two, at the tree.** The fruit splits and the newborn says the same lines the gnomes
speak in the play in the square. It takes the first name it is given. Preem pays the
party 5 gp for that name.
