# The mounted hunters — run sheet for David

The first fight of day one in [the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md): four Maalm
aristocrats on great hounds, with lances, against the party on foot in waist-high grass.
The full block is in the preserve file under *Maalm hunter*. The numbers to keep in front
of you:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hunter** | AC 16, 39 HP. **Lance** +4, reach 10 ft., 8 (1d12 + 2). **Hunting sword** +4, 6 (1d8 + 2). |
| **Charge** | Move 20 ft. straight at the target and hit it with the lance on the same turn: **+7 (2d6)**, and the target makes a **DC 13 Strength save or is knocked prone.** |
| **Hound** | AC 14, 37 HP, **Speed 60**. While ridden it can only Dash, Disengage or Dodge. Once its rider is dead it bites: +5, 10 (2d6 + 3), DC 13 Strength save or prone, with advantage when another hound or hunter is within 5 ft. of the target. |
| **Initiative** | Roll once for all four, at +1. They act together. |
| **The grass** | Difficult terrain for the party. The hounds cross it at full speed. |

## Every turn

The lance reaches 10 feet. A rider strikes from 10 feet away and the hound carries him
out again before anyone can swing back. A character with a sword has to reach the rider,
pull him off, or hit him at range.

The hound moves 60 feet, or 120 if it Dashes, and the rider can strike at any point in
that move. So the turn is: **60 feet in, one lance strike from 10 feet, 60 feet out.**
End every turn 70 feet or more from the nearest character. That is out of reach of a
60-foot spell. A bow or a 120-foot cantrip still reaches.

**Opportunity attacks.** A rider who stops at 10 feet never comes within 5 feet of anyone,
so riding out provokes nothing. Riding *through* the party does: every character within
5 feet of the hound's line gets an opportunity attack on the hound or the rider as it
passes. Go through only on turn 1.

## Turn by turn

**Before the first turn.** The party see four riders in a line, far out across the grass.
The riders see the rack, which stands taller than the grass. Roll initiative when the
riders are 70 feet out.

**Turn 1: through the party.** All four Dash. Each hound rides 60 feet in, the rider
strikes one character with the lance as he passes at 10 feet, and the hound carries on
through and out the far side, ending 60 feet past the party. Every rider takes a
different target, and the charge applies to all four strikes. Take the opportunity
attacks this once. At the end of the turn the party are on one side of the line, and the
riders are on the other, with whoever failed the save on the ground.

**Turn 2: pick one.** Look at the party and pick the one who looks weakest: on the
ground, in light armour, already bleeding, standing apart, or slow to get up. **All four
go at that one.** Each hound rides in from the far side, the rider strikes with the charge
from 10 feet, and the hound rides out again. Four lances at +4 land about two hits a
turn, and a charge hit is 15 damage.

A character who is prone changes the approach. A lance from 10 feet has disadvantage
against a prone target, so against a prone character the hound comes to 5 feet instead:
the lance's disadvantage inside 5 feet and the advantage against a prone target cancel,
and the roll is straight. Leaving 5 feet provokes, so that hound Disengages instead of
Dashing, and ends the turn 30 feet out.

**Turn 3 and after: the same.** Keep every lance on the same character until that
character is down, then pick the next weakest. The riders stay out of reach between
strikes every turn. They are here to kill the quarry and take the rack.

**A character at 0 hit points.** A rider who passes a downed character strikes it with
the lance as he passes: from 5 feet, an attack on an
unconscious target is an automatic critical hit, which is two failed death saves.
`[Will]` whether to run this as written, or have the riders ignore a downed character
while anyone is still standing.

## Exceptions

- **A hound cannot move.** *Entangle* or a Sentinel hit sets its speed to 0. The rider
  stays in the saddle and lances anyone who comes within 10 feet. When a character
  closes to 5 feet, he drops the lance, draws the sword, and fights from the saddle at
  +4 for 1d8 + 2. He dismounts only when the hound dies.
- ***Spike Growth.*** The first hound through it takes the damage for every 5 feet it
  crosses. The riders see what happened to it and the others ride around the area.
- **The hound is killed.** The rider uses his reaction to land on his feet, or falls prone
  within 5 feet of it. On foot he is in the grass at half speed. He drops the lance and
  fights with the sword where he stands.
- **The rider is killed.** The hound goes independent on the next round and bites the
  nearest character. It keeps fighting beside the other hounds.
- **The hound is shoved or dragged.** The rider makes a DC 10 Dexterity save or falls off,
  prone within 5 feet of the hound.
- **Readied attacks.** A character who readies an attack for when a rider comes within
  reach gets it. When a character readies, ride at a different character.

**They fight until they are dead.** The party cannot outrun the hounds, so the fight runs
to the end. A hunter at 5 hit points still rides in.
