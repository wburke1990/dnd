# PART ONE — BACKGROUND
*Read in prep.*

---

> Part of the Brauron quest — the town the party comes in through, at the edge of the
> poison deadland. See [the hell-tree](the-hell-tree.md) (the tree itself) and
> [the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md) (the hunt).

**Brauron** is the last town before the poison deadland, on the coast north of
[Raand](../../../nila/lore/nila-atlas.md). It is a town of **gnome artisans and
performers descended from dragons**, where the hunters and pilgrims gather and where the **priestesses of
Artemis** keep their temple. It was founded four thousand years ago by
[Momotarō](../../../../handouts/momotaro.md), a newborn demon out of a fruit of
[the hell-tree](the-hell-tree.md). The fruit came down the river out of the deadland to
an old gnome couple, who gave him water and raised him
([the founding](../lore/the-founding-of-brauron.md)). A peach grove rings the town, his
statue stands in the square, and the gnomes act out stories of children born from fruit
in the streets.

`[name]` Brauron is named for the real Artemis sanctuary where girls served the goddess
as bears. The town keeps that rite. The name is Greek. The Norse map-names (Raand, Aar, Suartleheim) came with
the Muspel empire, and this town is older. Lonka and Kalikhat also keep older
names.

---

## The people

Gnome artisans and performers, descended from dragons, as in [Raand](../../../nila/lore/nila-atlas.md).
They carve, build, and act. A child born from a fruit is their holy story.

---

## The Megabyzoi and the Attis mystery

`[DM background]` The Megabyzoi are real: Artemis's priests at Ephesus were castrated,
in the manner of the **Galli**, the priests of Cybele and Attis, who castrated themselves. Attis
castrates himself under the pine and dies.
The eunuch priests keep the Attis story.

## Read aloud — the square

> *The town is ringed with peach trees, the branches loaded with fruit. In the square a stone statue
> of a boy stands on a plinth, with writing carved into the base. In front of it,
> players in painted masks and robes are acting something out for a small crowd — an
> old couple, a great peach, a child stepping out of it.*

The founding: **[Momotarō](../../../../handouts/momotaro.md)**. An old gnome couple find a
peach in the river and a boy steps out of it. They raise him, and the town is built
around their house. The gnomes act it in the street in kabuki masks; the statue is Momotarō; the
passage where the peach splits is cut into the base.

`[DM]` Momotarō was a demon out of [the hell-tree](the-hell-tree.md). See [the founding of Brauron](../lore/the-founding-of-brauron.md).

`[DM]` The line on the base — *"Wait a bit, old man!"* — is one of the lines a newborn
demon says at [the hell-tree](the-hell-tree.md). Read it out here; the same line is used
again at the tree.

---

## Read aloud — the fountain

> *A fountain stands in the square. Three stone maidens are carved at the spout, mouths
> open and hands out, and the water runs down over them. The townsfolk fill their jugs
> here.*

**[The Three Citrons](../../../../handouts/the-three-citrons.md):** a maiden bursts from a cut
citron begging for water, and vanishes if she is not given it at once. The fountain
carving shows that moment.

`[DM]` At [the hell-tree](the-hell-tree.md) a newborn demon asks for water, and
hardens if the water is slow. The fountain here runs day and night.

---

## Read aloud — the puppet show

> *In a doorway off the square a puppet show is going for the children. A puppet plants
> a seed; a paper flower opens; a tiny puppet girl sits up inside it.*

**[Thumbelina](../../../../handouts/thumbelina.md):** a planted barleycorn grows into a flower
with a tiny girl inside.

## Read aloud — the temple of Artemis

> *The temple is dim and cool. The women who keep it are veiled and still; among them
> stand a few men with smooth faces. On the walls, in paint, a young man is
> gored by a boar, figures stand over his body, and a tree grows where his blood falls.*

**Two priesthoods keep the temple, modelled on those at Ephesus.** The **bee-priestesses (the
Melissae, "the bees")** are Artemis's virgin women, and they run the hunt. The **eunuch
priests (the Megabyzoi)** keep the **[Attis mystery](../../../../handouts/the-birth-of-attis.md)**.
Attis is a young god born from an almond. The almond tree grew where a killed god's blood
fell. Attis dies, and he is mourned. The party sees the Attis story only here, painted on the temple walls.

The priestesses also serve Artemis as **Hecate**, of the moon and the dead. The
deadland and the tree are hers under that name. The town's
girls serve the goddess as **bears** before they marry (the real Brauron rite).

---

## The hunt starts here — dressing the quarry as the stag

The cult runs the sacred hunt out in the wild — two hunts, one for beasts and one for a
man, after Actaeon (see [the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md)) — and it is set up
here, in the temple. The cult dresses its people in the skins of the goddess's beasts:
the girls are her **bears**, the quarry is her **stag** (antlers and a deer-hide), and
the hunters are her **hounds** (hound-pelts).

A party that goes in as the quarry signs on here. When a priestess takes down a name, she asks first
**where to send the quarry's pay.** Most quarry die in the hunt. The man-hunt is a sacrifice to Artemis. At Aulis a deer was put on the
altar in Iphigenia's place. Here the quarry is dressed as a deer and hunted.

**The full rite is in [the dressing](dressing-the-stag.md)** — the sign-up, the water,
the barley, the hair cut and burned, the crown, the antlers, the roll of the hounds,
the two readings and the Longstrider at the door. Run that doc from here.
