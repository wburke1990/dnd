# The Brass Jackals at the ford — run sheet for David

The fight after [the mounted hunters](the-mounted-hunters-run-sheet.md) in
[the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md). The party ride the charmed hounds from
[session 18](../../../../sessions/session-18.md) and reach a river. On the far bank
[the Brass Jackals](../../../../characters/the-brass-jackals.md) are fighting a shambling
mound. Sefi is inside the mound, and a crocodile has Pim by the leg. Two tigers circle in
the grass. Two more crocodiles wait in the ford.

The party come into this fight hurt and short of spell slots. Weapons and cantrips can win it.
Expect the party to take about one round of heavy damage between them. They can also
ride on and leave the Jackals to fight alone.

| **Crocodile** | AC 12, 19 HP. Speed 20, **swim 30**. **Bite** +4, 7 (1d10 + 2), and the target is grappled (escape DC 12). While it holds something it bites nothing else. |

## The ground

- **The ford** is 30 feet wide and waist-deep: difficult terrain. A character on foot
  spends a whole Dash crossing it.
- **The water is poison.** A creature that wades in makes a **DC 10 Constitution save** or
  is poisoned until the fight ends. A rider in the saddle stays dry.
- **The mound** stands 20 feet back from the far bank, in the reeds. Pim's crocodile is on
  the bank to its left.
- **The grass** on the far side is waist-high. The tigers move through it at 60 feet out.

## The crossing

A ridden hound that Dashes has 120 feet of movement. The ford costs 60 of it and the 20
feet up to the mound costs 20, so **a rider who crosses on round 1 still gets an attack
on the mound that turn.**

**Two crocodiles lie under the water in the middle of the ford.** From the bank the ford
is brown water and reeds. Each crocodile takes an **opportunity attack** on the first hound
or character to pass out of its reach: one per crocodile per round, so two in all. A
crocodile bites the hound, not the rider.

**Spotting the crocodiles.** As a rider's hound goes into the water, the rider rolls
**Wisdom (Perception), DC 12** (the crocodile's Stealth +2, plus 10). The roll is free.

- **Success:** the rider sees two eyes and a snout break the surface a few feet ahead. The
  crocodile's bite on that hound is a normal roll.
- **Failure:** the jaws close on the hound before the rider spots the crocodile. The bite has **advantage**.

A rider who sees one can shout a warning to the riders behind. Each of them then rolls the check
with advantage.

A bitten hound is grappled and its speed is 0. It stays in the river, with its rider on
it. The rider can stay in the saddle, or get off into the water and make the poison save.
To free the hound, its rider or a character next to it uses an action on a DC 12 Strength
(Athletics) check, or kills the crocodile.

Riders behind a crocodile that holds a hound pass it safely this round.

After the crossing the two crocodiles stay in the river. They bite anything that comes
within 5 feet of the water or into it.

bonus and damage; the table gives the AC they roll against.

water and bites him again. The party cross, take the opportunity attacks, and reach the
far bank. Anyone with a bow or a ranged cantrip can shoot the mound from the near bank.

about 38 damage a round between them against AC 15. The rolls are the same as round 2.

### Getting Sefi out

- **Kill the mound.** It drops Sefi.
- **Pull her out.** A creature next to the mound uses its action: **DC 14 Strength
  (Athletics)**. On a success Sefi lands prone next to it.
- **Sefi gets out herself.** On her turn she makes the same check, at DC 14.

**The mound holds one creature at a time, It holds Sefi now.** Once she is out,
a rider hit by both slams is pulled from the saddle and engulfed. Hounds are Large, too big
for the mound to engulf.

### The mound

It slams whatever is closest, rider or Jackal. It stays in the reeds while Sefi is
in it. Once she is out, it moves toward the nearest Medium creature and tries to engulf it.

### The tigers

They stay in the grass, 60 feet out, and walk circles around the fight. A tiger attacks
only:

- a creature at 0 HP,
- a creature with no ally within 10 feet,
- a hound with no rider, or
- Pim, once he is in the water.

**Pounce on a hound.** A hound knocked prone throws its rider: DC 10 Dexterity save or
the rider falls prone 5 feet from it. Or the rider uses their reaction to step off and
land on their feet.

**When a tiger takes 15 damage or more, both tigers go back into the grass** and stay
there for the rest of the fight.

## If the party ride on

The mound kills Sefi on round 3 or 4. The crocodile drowns Pim. Mogg, Hadda and Veska
fall back from the bank into the grass, and the tigers follow them.

## Changing it at the table

- **Easier:** lower the mound's hit points first, then take one crocodile out of the ford.
- **Harder:** add a third tiger. Then a third crocodile on the far bank.

**Difficulty.** Two crocodiles in the ford, one on the bank, two tigers and the mound come
to 2,500 XP. Six monsters against nine on the party's side (five level-4 characters and
four level-3 Jackals) count ×1.5, which is 3,750. That is between hard (2,775) and deadly
(4,100) for fresh characters. The mound starts at 80 HP and the tigers mostly hold back,
so at the table it plays closer to a fight with the mound alone.
