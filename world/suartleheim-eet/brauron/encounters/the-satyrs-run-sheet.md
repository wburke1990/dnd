# The satyrs — run sheet for David

The second day's fight in [the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md). The satyrs the party
met in the peach grove outside Brauron ([session 17](../../../../sessions/session-17.md))
enter the preserve behind them. They came for the party.

**Eight satyrs, one of them with pipes.** Eight at CR 1/2 is 2,000 XP adjusted for a party
of five: between hard and deadly at level 4, and it leaves room in the day's 8,500 for
the beasts.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Satyr** | AC 14, 31 HP, **Speed 40**. Stealth +5, Perception +2. **Magic Resistance**: advantage on saves against spells. |
| **Shortbow** | +5 to hit, range 80/320, 6 (1d6 + 3). |
| **Shortsword** | +5 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3). |
| **Ram** | +3 to hit, 6 (2d4 + 1), when they close. |
| **The pipes** | One satyr carries them. As an action, every creature within 60 feet that can hear makes a **DC 13 Wisdom save**, and the piper picks the tune: **charmed** for 1 minute, **frightened** for 1 minute, or **asleep** for 1 minute (a sleeper wakes when hurt or shaken). A creature that saves is immune to that tune for 24 hours. |
| **Initiative** | Roll once for all eight, at +3. |
| **The ground** | A stand of the low knotted trees, clear underneath, with the waist-high grass all round it. The grass is difficult terrain for the party. The satyrs move at full speed under the trees. |

## Every turn

The satyrs stay in the trees and shoot. The party are in the grass at half speed, so a
character needs two or three turns to reach the trees, and a satyr needs one turn to fall
back 40 feet through them. When a character gets within 30 feet of a satyr, that satyr
moves 40 feet back into the trees and shoots from there.

Six bows fire every turn. Six shortbows at +5 land three or four hits a turn, about 20
damage, on one character.

The piper plays instead of shooting. The eighth satyr keeps a shortsword out and stands
between the piper and the party.

## Turn by turn

**Before the first turn.** The party hear the pipes from the trees before they see
anyone. The satyrs have Stealth +5 and are under the trees; a character who looks for
them rolls Perception against that. Roll initiative when the first arrow comes.

**Turn 1: the lullaby.** The piper plays the lullaby at the character who does the most damage. Every
character within 60 feet saves. Six bows go at whoever fails: attacks on a sleeping target
have advantage.

**Turn 2: the same target.** Bows on the sleeper until the sleeper wakes. The piper plays
the frightening strain on the characters who are still awake and coming through the
grass: a frightened character cannot move closer to the piper. A character who saved on
turn 1 is immune to the lullaby, so the piper changes the tune each turn.

**Turn 3 and after: back and shoot.** Any satyr with a character within 30 feet falls
back 40 feet through the trees, then shoots. The rest keep shooting at the character
with the least armour, or the one on the ground. The piper plays whichever tune the
fewest characters have saved against.

## Exceptions

- **A character reaches a satyr.** That satyr rams and draws the shortsword. Two more
  satyrs close on the same
  character and the rest keep shooting. A satyr at 10 hit points or under walks away
  through the trees instead.
- **The party go round the trees instead of through the grass.** The satyrs move through
  the stand to keep the trees between themselves and the party. The stand is about 200
  feet across.
- ***Entangle* or *Spike Growth* on the trees.** The satyrs have Magic Resistance and roll
  the save with advantage. A satyr that is restrained is left where it is by the others.
  A satyr that walks into Spike Growth takes the damage; the rest go round, the same as
  the hounds.
- **The party set the trees on fire.** The trees are dry and the grass is dry. The
  satyrs leave the stand and the fight is over, and the fire is between the party and
  the gate.
- **The party stand still and shoot back.** The satyrs have the better cover and the
  better bows. A party that stands in the open grass and shoots back takes more damage
  than it deals.

**They break off when three are down.** When three of the eight are dead or on the
ground, the rest fall back through the trees at 40 feet a turn and are gone. The satyrs
outrun the party in the grass. `[Will]` Decide whether they come back that night, or at
the tree.
