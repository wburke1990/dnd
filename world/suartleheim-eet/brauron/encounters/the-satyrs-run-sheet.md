# The satyrs — run sheet for David

The second day's fight in [the game preserve](the-game-preserve.md). The satyrs the party
met in the peach grove outside Brauron ([session 17](../../../../sessions/session-17.md))
are a hunting party like the Maalm aristocrats: mounted on great hounds, in the preserve
for the hunt. They came for the party.

**Eight satyrs on eight hounds, one satyr with pipes.** Eight at CR 1/2 is 2,000 XP
adjusted for a party of five: between hard and deadly at level 4, and it leaves room in
the day's 8,500 for the beasts. A hound that fights on after its rider dies adds CR 1
(200 XP), the same as the hunters' hounds.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Satyr** | AC 14, 31 HP, Speed 40 on foot, **60 mounted**. Stealth +5, Perception +2. **Magic Resistance**: advantage on saves against spells. Only the satyr has it. |
| **Shortbow** | +5 to hit, range 80/320, 6 (1d6 + 3). Shot from the saddle at any point in the hound's move. |
| **Shortsword** | +5 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3). |
| **Ram** | +3 to hit, 6 (2d4 + 1), on foot. |
| **The pipes** | One satyr carries them. As an action, every creature within 60 feet that can hear makes a **DC 13 Wisdom save**, and the piper picks the tune: **charmed** for 1 minute, **frightened** for 1 minute, or **asleep** for 1 minute (a sleeper wakes when hurt or shaken). A creature that saves is immune to that tune for 24 hours. |
| **Hound** | AC 14, 37 HP, **Speed 60**. While ridden it can only Dash, Disengage or Dodge. Once its rider is dead it bites: +5, 10 (2d6 + 3), DC 13 Strength save or prone, with advantage when another hound or satyr is within 5 ft. of the target. |
| **Initiative** | Roll once for all eight, at +3. |
| **The ground** | Open grass, waist-high. Difficult terrain for the party. The hounds cross it at full speed. |

The mounted rules are in the preserve file under *The mounted rules, in short*, and the
hunters' sheet has the opportunity-attack and prone details:
[the mounted hunters run sheet](the-mounted-hunters-run-sheet.md).

## Every turn

The hunters close with lances. The satyrs stay out and shoot. A hound moves 60 feet, or
120 if it Dashes, and the satyr shoots once from the saddle at any point in that move.
So the turn is: **ride in to 80 feet, one arrow, ride back out.** End every turn 80 feet
or more from the nearest character: inside the shortbow's range, outside a 60-foot
spell's. A longbow or a 120-foot cantrip still reaches.

They ride in a loose ring round the party and shoot inward. A character in the
waist-high grass covers 15 feet a turn, and a hound at 60 feet stays out of reach. Seven bows at +5 land about four hits a turn, about 25 damage, on one
character.

The piper rides to 60 feet, plays, and the hound carries him back out to 80 on the same
turn.

## Turn by turn

**Before the first turn.** The party see the ring of riders form in the grass, out past
bow range. Roll initiative when the first rider turns inward.

**Turn 1: the lullaby.** The piper rides to 60 feet and plays the lullaby. Every
character within 60 feet saves. Seven arrows go at whoever fails: attacks on a sleeping
target have advantage. The piper's hound carries him back out.

**Turn 2: the same target.** Seven arrows go at the sleeper until he wakes. The piper
rides in and plays the frightening strain on the characters who are awake: a frightened
character cannot move closer to the piper. A character who saved on turn 1 is immune to
the lullaby, so the piper changes the tune each turn.

**Turn 3 and after: the ring.** Keep riding the ring. Arrows go on the character with
the least armour, or the one on the ground. The piper plays whichever tune the fewest
characters have saved against. A rider who takes an arrow or a spell moves to the far
side of the ring from the character who shot or cast it.

## Exceptions

- **A hound cannot move.** *Entangle* or a Sentinel hit sets its speed to 0. The satyr
  stays in the saddle and keeps shooting from where it is. When a character closes to
  5 feet, he drops the bow and draws the shortsword. He dismounts only when the hound
  dies.
- ***Spike Growth.*** The first hound through it takes the damage for every 5 feet it
  crosses. The others ride round the area.
- **The hound is killed.** The satyr uses his reaction to land on his feet, or falls
  prone within 5 feet of it. On foot in the grass he moves 20 feet a turn. He walks away
  from the party and keeps shooting.
- **The rider is killed.** The hound goes independent on the next round and bites the
  nearest character. It keeps fighting beside the other hounds.
- **The hound is shoved or dragged.** The satyr makes a DC 10 Dexterity save or falls off,
  prone within 5 feet of the hound.
- **A character reaches a satyr on foot.** He rams, draws the shortsword, and fights.
  Two riders come in to 10 feet and shoot the character from the saddle.
- **Readied attacks.** A character who readies a shot for when a rider comes within range
  gets it. When a character readies, the riders come in on the far side of the ring from
  that character.
- **The party lie down in the grass.** A character lying prone in waist-high grass is
  heavily obscured from a rider at 80 feet. The riders come in to 30 feet to shoot, and a
  character who stands up reaches them in one move.

**They try to break off when three are down.** When three of the eight satyrs are dead
or on the ground, the rest turn their hounds and ride at 120 feet a turn. A satyr whose
hound is restrained, entangled, or dead stays where it is, and the party can catch it. A
satyr on a hound that can still move outruns anyone in the grass. Play the retreat out,
turn by turn, the same as the fight.
