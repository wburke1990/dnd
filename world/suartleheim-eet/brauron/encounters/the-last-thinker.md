# The Last Thinker — stat block and fight

> **DM reference.** The one fight on the
> **[Island of the Thinkers](island-of-the-thinkers.md)** — five player characters at
> level 3 against one halfling. **David plays him.** Part One is what he wants and how
> he is played; Part Two is the stat block and the spell list; Part Three is the fight,
> round by round. Legend: `[run]` · `[settling]` · `[OPEN]`.

---

# PART ONE — WHAT THE FIGHT IS OVER

## The trigger `[settling — Will 9/15]`

The storm broke the mast. The island has one tree on it. The mast the party sail away
with in the run sheet is **freshly hewn**, so the tree comes down either way — the fight
decides whether he hands it over or they take it off him.

He offers them everything else first: water from the cistern, the garden, the beams in
the hall under the hill, a bed. He will not offer the tree, and he does not bargain over
it. **The first axe laid against the trunk starts the fight.**

`[OPEN]` If you want a different trigger — they go down to the bell against his word, or
they open his statue, or Pax says something about the flat world at the wrong moment —
the stat block and the spell list run the same. Only the round-one script below assumes
the tree.

## How David plays him

- **He is not trying to kill them.** Every opening move keeps bodies away from the trunk
  and does no damage on its own. He kills only after the tree is cut, and he gives them a
  round of warning before each escalation.
- **He never goes more than 60 feet from the tree.** An archer standing off at 150 feet
  can shoot him all fight without being reached.
- **He casts no fire.** Not *Flaming Sphere*, not *Heat Metal* on Jasper's armour, not
  *Wall of Fire* — nothing with a flame in it, at any point, however badly the fight is
  going. The tree would burn.
- **He speaks Halfling and Giant, and no Common.** Blackacre's Floating Fleet was
  majority halfling — ask Sam whether he took the language, rather than deciding it for
  him. With nobody to interpret, he negotiates by pointing: the garden, the cistern,
  the broken mast.

Four lines, and he does not have many more:

> *"Water's in the cistern. Food in the garden."*

> *"Not the tree."*

> *"Put it down."*

And at 0 hit points:

> *"Ring the bell under the hill."*

# PART TWO — STAT BLOCK

*Small humanoid (halfling) · a druid 9 build · effective challenge ~6 — above the deadly
line for five level-3 characters, which is the intent. The ability array and the spell
picks are a defensible build; retune to taste.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Armor Class** | 16 (stone-set hide) |
| **Hit Points** | 115 (18d8 + 36) |
| **Speed** | 25 ft. |
| **Initiative** | +2 |
| **Proficiency Bonus** | +4 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) |

- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +7, Wis +8
- **Skills** Insight +8, Medicine +8, Nature +7, Perception +8, Survival +8
- **Passive Perception** 18
- **Languages** Halfling, Giant
- **Spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks**

**A druid 9 rolls 67 hit points.** Five level-3 characters do 45 to 50 a round when they
all connect, so 67 is two rounds and no fight. 115 lasts four or five rounds. Adjust that
number and nothing else if the fight runs long or short.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** When he fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed
instead. *Hold Person*, *Sleep* and a shove off the hill are what these are for.

**Shillelagh.** His staff counts as magical while the cantrip is up: +8 to hit, reach
5 ft., **8 (1d8 + 4)** bludgeoning.

**The Ancestor (1/Day, action).** For 10 minutes he is **Large**, gains **45 temporary hit
points**, his Speed becomes 40 ft., and he makes **two slams** a turn: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., **11 (1d12 + 5)** bludgeoning. He
can still cast. He does not change shape — the same halfling at four times the size.

**Reaction — the ground rises (1/round).** When he is hit by an attack he can see, the
ground or a nearby statue comes up: **+5 AC against that attack**, which can turn it into
a miss.

## Legendary actions

Two a round, spent at the end of another creature's turn, refreshed at the start of his.

- **Step (1).** He teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see within 5
  feet of any Thinker statue. There are nine on the hilltop, so this is most of the
  hilltop.
- **Thorn Whip (1).** He casts the cantrip: ranged spell attack +8, 30 ft., **7 (2d6)**
  piercing, and the target is pulled 10 feet toward him. **This is how somebody ends up in
  the** ***Spike Growth*** **without choosing to walk into it.**
- **Root the ground (2).** Every creature within 10 feet of him or of the tree makes a DC
  16 Strength save or is restrained until the end of its next turn.

# THE SPELL LIST — for David

Druid 9 slots: **4 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 1**. He can concentrate on only one of the starred
spells at a time, and a hit on him forces a Constitution save at DC 10 or half the damage,
whichever is higher, at **+6**.

**Cantrips.** *Thorn Whip* · *Shillelagh* · *Mold Earth* · *Druidcraft* · *Guidance*

**1st (4 slots).** *Entangle*\* · *Faerie Fire*\* · *Fog Cloud*\* · *Goodberry* ·
*Absorb Elements*

**2nd (3 slots).** ***Spike Growth***\* · *Moonbeam*\* · *Pass Without Trace*\* ·
*Barkskin* · *Hold Person*\*

**3rd (3 slots).** ***Erupting Earth*** · *Call Lightning*\* · *Meld into Stone* ·
*Plant Growth* · *Sleet Storm*\* · *Wind Wall*\*

**4th (3 slots).** ***Stoneskin***\* · *Stone Shape* · *Grasping Vine*\* · *Blight*

**5th (1 slot).** ***Wall of Stone***\*

What each of the five bolded ones is for:

- ***Spike Growth*** — the ground between them and the trunk, 20-foot radius, 2d4 for
  every 5 feet moved through it, and the ground looks unchanged. Round one.
- ***Erupting Earth*** — 3d12 in a 20-foot cube, Dexterity save for half, **no
  concentration**, so he keeps the spikes up while he casts it. Round two.
- ***Wall of Stone*** — a ring of panels around the trunk, or a wall between the axe and
  the tree. His one 5th-level slot, and it costs him the spikes.
- ***Stoneskin*** — resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Against
  their weapons, all of them nonmagical, this halves most of the damage they do. **Hold it
  back** until he has lost half his hit points; it ends the fight early if he opens with
  it.
- ***Call Lightning*** — 3d10 a round at range, once he is losing. 4d10 if you keep the
  storm overhead; the run sheet has it past, so 3d10.

*Blight* is on the list because a druid 9 has it. He would not cast it here, and there is
nothing on the island to cast it on but the tree.

# PART THREE — THE FIGHT

## The ground

The hilltop is a flat sixty feet across. The tree stands at the middle of it with the
garden around its roots, thirty feet of turned soil and a cistern. Nine Thinker statues
stand at the edge of the flat, none higher than a man's waist, the newest of them his own.
The cave mouth is on the north side. The slope up is loose stone and the path through it
is the only easy footing, and he knows where it is.

## Round one — what he does `[run]`

| | Init | AC | First move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Last Thinker** | +2 | 16 | Steps between the party and the trunk, casts ***Spike Growth*** on the path |

- **Action: *Spike Growth*** over the loose stone between the party and the tree. It does
  nothing until somebody moves, and it does not look like difficult ground. A DC 16 Wisdom
  (Perception) check spots the spikes before somebody walks in.
- **Movement:** he puts himself between the party and the trunk and stays there.
- **Legendary actions: *Thorn Whip*** on whoever is nearest the tree, twice if the first
  one misses. Ten feet toward him is ten feet back down the slope, and it is 2d4 of spikes
  on the way if the pull crosses them.
- **He does not attack anyone in round one.** If nobody has swung at the tree yet, he
  spends his legendary actions on *Thorn Whip* only against whoever is holding the axe.

## Round two

- **Action: *Erupting Earth*** under the two closest to the tree. 3d12, Dexterity save at
  DC 16 for half, and the ground it hits stays difficult. He keeps *Spike Growth* up while
  he does it — that is the reason it is this spell and not *Call Lightning*.
- **Legendary: Step** to a statue behind the party, then ***Thorn Whip*** from there. He
  is now on the far side of them from the tree. To face him, they have to turn away from
  the trunk.
- **If Aniess has gone invisible or hidden:** ***Faerie Fire*** instead of *Erupting
  Earth*, on the twenty-foot cube she was last in.

## Round three, or the moment the tree is struck — whichever comes first

- **Action: *Wall of Stone***. Ten panels, ten feet by ten. He puts them in a ring around
  the trunk, or across it as a wall with the axe on the wrong side. This costs him *Spike
  Growth*, and the party's footing opens up.
- **After the wall, he is trying to hurt them.**

## Round four

- **Action: *The Ancestor***. The halfling stands up Large, with 45 temporary hit points,
  and comes down off the garden wall. Two slams a turn at 1d12 + 5, reach 10 feet. Play him
  as the same man, larger.
- **If he is under half his hit points by now, *Stoneskin* first**, and the Ancestor on
  round five.

## Round five and after

- ***Call Lightning***, once a round, from wherever he is standing, at whoever is nearest
  the tree.
- ***Meld into Stone*** into the rock of the hilltop when three of them have him
  surrounded. Nothing reaches him in there, and it costs him his next turn to step back
  out. His own statue is too small to hold him.
- ***Hold Person*** on Jasper.

## How it ends

**He stops fighting if:**

- They step away from the tree and put the axe down. He does not pursue, and the offer of
  the cistern and the garden and the hall beams stands.
- Anybody mends the tree, waters it, or puts a seed in the soil in front of him. Blackacre
  can grow and repair timber. That ends the fight.
- They get him to half hit points and then stop. He has been waiting a long time for
  anyone at all to come.

**At 0 hit points** he is dying, not dead — stabilise him and he lives, and either way he
gets his last words out. The rest of the session is unchanged: the bell, the hall, the
island going under, the dragon coming out of the water
([the island of the thinkers](island-of-the-thinkers.md), Under the Hill).

## Dials

- **Too hard:** 90 hit points, one Legendary Resistance, no *Stoneskin*, and skip the
  Ancestor.
- **Too easy:** three legendary actions instead of two, keep the storm overhead for 4d10
  *Call Lightning*, and open round one with *Stoneskin* already up.
- **Five against one is five turns to his one.** The two legendary actions and the
  reaction are what let him fight five characters; if David forgets them for a round, hand
  them back.
