# The Last Thinker — stat block and fight

> **DM reference.** The one fight on the
> **[Island of the Thinkers](island-of-the-thinkers.md)** — five player characters at
> level 3 against one halfling. **David plays him.** Part One is what the fight is over
> and how he is played; Part Two is the stat block and the spell list; Part Three is the
> fight, round by round. Legend: `[run]` · `[settling]` · `[OPEN]`.

---

# PART ONE — WHAT THE FIGHT IS OVER

## The mast `[new — Will 9/15]`

The storm snapped the mast, and there is one tree on the island. **Nobody in the party can
replace a mast with magic at level 3.**

- **Mending** repairs a break no bigger than a foot across. A snapped mast is not that.
- **Blackacre's concept is that a druid grows and repairs timber**, and it is why the
  Fleet valued him. There is no spell on a druid 3's list that does it. If Sam asks, that
  is the honest answer, and it is the reason they need the tree.
- **Jasper is the ship's carpenter** (session 11). Given a felled trunk and carpenter's
  tools he can shape a spar and step it: a day's work, DC 13 with the tools. `[run]`
- **Nothing else on the island is tall enough.** The statues are stone, and the driftwood
  on the beach is too short.

So they need his tree, and he will not give it.

## The trigger `[settling]`

He offers what he has: water from the cistern, food out of the garden, a place to sleep.
He does not bargain over the tree. **The first axe laid against the trunk starts the
fight.** After that they are fighting him.

`[OPEN]` If something else starts it instead, the stat block and the spell list run the
same. Only the round-one script assumes he is standing between them and the trunk.

## How David plays him

- **He is not trying to kill them.** His first two rounds do nothing but keep them off
  him and off the tree. He escalates a step at a time, and he gives them a round to back
  off before each step.
- **He never goes more than 60 feet from the tree.** An archer standing off at 150 feet
  can shoot him all fight without being reached, and if the party retreat down the slope
  he does not follow.
- **He speaks Giant and Halfling, and no Common.** **Sarric speaks Giant** — a firbolg
  does, and in Nila the firbolgs are giant-kin
  ([lineages](../../../nila/lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md)), so the conversation runs
  through Jeremy. Blackacre may have Halfling off the Fleet; ask Sam rather than deciding
  it for him. With no interpreter at all, he points: the garden, the cistern, the broken
  mast.

Four lines, and he does not have many more:

> *"Water's in the cistern. Food in the garden."*

> *"Not the tree."*

> *"Put it down."*

And at 0 hit points:

> *"Ring the bell under the hill."*

# PART TWO — STAT BLOCK

*Small humanoid (halfling) · a druid 9 build · effective challenge ~6 — above the deadly
encounter threshold for five level-3 characters, which is the intent. The ability array and the spell
picks are a defensible build; retune to taste.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Armor Class** | 16 (stone-set hide) |
| **Hit Points** | 115 (18d8 + 36) |
| **Speed** | 25 ft. |
| **Initiative** | +2 |
| **Proficiency Bonus** | +4 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) |

- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +7, Wis +8
- **Skills** Insight +8, Medicine +8, Nature +7, Perception +8, Survival +8
- **Passive Perception** 18
- **Languages** Giant, Halfling
- **Spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks**

**A druid 9 rolls 67 hit points.** Five level-3 characters do 45 to 50 a round when they
all connect, so at 67 he would go down in two rounds. 115 lasts four or five rounds. Adjust that
number and nothing else if the fight runs long or short.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** When he fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed
instead. *Hold Person*, *Sleep* and a shove off the hill are what these are for.

**Shillelagh.** His staff counts as magical while the cantrip is up: +8 to hit, reach
5 ft., **8 (1d8 + 4)** bludgeoning. He casts it before the party reach the top, so it is
up from round one.

**The Ancestor (1/Day, action).** For 10 minutes he is **Large**, gains **45 temporary hit
points**, his Speed becomes 40 ft., and he makes **two slams** a turn: +8 to hit, reach
10 ft., **11 (1d12 + 5)** bludgeoning. He can still cast. He does not change shape — the
same halfling at four times the size.

**No Wild Shape.** A druid 9 has it; he does not use it. The Ancestor is the only shape he
takes.

**Reaction — the ground rises (1/round).** When he is hit by an attack he can see, the
ground or a nearby statue comes up: **+5 AC against that attack**, which can turn it into
a miss. *Absorb Elements* is also a reaction, and he gets one a round, not both.

## His bonus action

- **Shillelagh**, once, before or at the start of the fight.
- ***Healing Word*** on himself — 1d4 + 4, at range, and it works while somebody has hold
  of him. This is most of what his 1st-level slots are for.
- `[DM]` **One levelled spell a turn.** A turn he casts *Healing Word* with his bonus
  action is a turn his action can only be a cantrip — *Thorn Whip*, or a swing with the
  staff. This is the same rule the Lapis Writ run on in the bath house.

## Legendary actions

Two a round, spent at the end of another creature's turn, refreshed at the start of his.

- **Step (1).** He teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see within 5 feet
  of a Thinker statue. **Needs at least three statues spread around the hilltop** for this
  to be worth anything — see *The map* below.
- **Thorn Whip (1).** He casts the cantrip: ranged spell attack +8, 30 ft., **7 (2d6)**
  piercing, and the target is pulled 10 feet toward him. `[DM]` **Forced movement sets off
  *Spike Growth*** — the spell says a creature that moves into or within the area, and
  being dragged is moving. This is how somebody takes the spikes without walking in.
- **Root the ground (2).** Every creature within 10 feet of him makes a DC 16 Strength
  save or is restrained until the end of its next turn.

# THE SPELL LIST — for David

Druid 9 slots: **4 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 1**. He can concentrate on only one of the starred
spells at a time, and a hit on him forces a Constitution save at DC 10 or half the damage,
whichever is higher, at **+6**.

**Cantrips.** *Thorn Whip* · *Shillelagh* · *Mold Earth* · *Druidcraft* · *Guidance*

**1st (4 slots).** *Healing Word* · *Entangle*\* · *Faerie Fire*\* · *Fog Cloud*\* ·
*Absorb Elements* · *Goodberry*

**2nd (3 slots).** ***Spike Growth***\* · *Moonbeam*\* · *Hold Person*\* · *Barkskin* ·
*Pass Without Trace*\*

**3rd (3 slots).** ***Erupting Earth*** · *Call Lightning*\* · *Meld into Stone* ·
*Plant Growth* · *Sleet Storm*\* · *Wind Wall*\*

**4th (3 slots).** ***Stoneskin***\* · *Stone Shape* · *Grasping Vine*\*

**5th (1 slot).** ***Wall of Stone***\*

What each of the five bolded ones is for:

- ***Spike Growth*** — 20-foot radius, 2d4 for every 5 feet moved through it, and the
  ground looks unchanged. He centres it on himself and then stands still. Round one.
- ***Erupting Earth*** — 3d12 in a 20-foot cube, Dexterity save for half, **no
  concentration**, so the spikes stay up while he casts it. Round two.
- ***Stoneskin*** — resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Their
  weapons are all nonmagical, so this halves most of the damage they do, and the halved
  damage lowers the DC of his concentration saves. **Hold it back** until somebody is in
  melee with him.
- ***Wall of Stone*** — ten panels, ten feet by ten. Across the hilltop it cuts the party
  in two; around the trunk it puts the tree out of reach. His one 5th-level slot.
- ***Call Lightning*** — 3d10 a round at range, once he is losing. 4d10 if you keep the
  storm overhead; the run sheet has the storm already past, so 3d10.

**The concentration order is *Spike Growth* → *Stoneskin* → *Call Lightning*.** He can
hold only one at a time; casting the next one ends the one before it.

# PART THREE — THE FIGHT

## The map `[settling]`

Custom is the right call. **Nothing in the opened OneWorld library is a bare grass summit
with one tree** ([the map inventory](../../../../tts/one-world-maps-inventory.md)), and
the 272 unopened maps are names only, so *Grandfather Tree* and *Low Hill* are guesses
until somebody imports them. What is worth taking from the opened ones:

- **Small town** (`189d29`) — its grass floor image is already fixed and Steam-hosted. The
  ground texture to reuse.
- **Valience Farmhouse** (`c3aa13`) — the hut. A pruned bag is already stashed at
  `map-stash/farmhouse_clean_c3aa13.json`.
- **Haagen** (`340cba`) — statues, a fountain and a monument, and zero dead URLs of 35
  probed. The prop donor if you want more stone than you have.

What the fight needs on it: **a flat about 60 feet across**, the tree at the middle with
turned soil and a cistern around its roots, the hut or the cave mouth to one side, and the
**statues scattered where you like — small, none over three feet tall, each a different
size.** Three or four is plenty. They are solid rock with nothing inside them; what they
do in the fight is give him somewhere to Step to.

## Round one — what he does `[run]`

| | Init | AC | First move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Last Thinker** | +2 | 16 | Stands where he is and casts ***Spike Growth*** on himself |

- **Action: *Spike Growth*,** centred on himself, 20-foot radius. He does not move again
  while he holds it. Anything that walks in to swing at him pays 2d4 per 5 feet, and the
  ground looks no different — a DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the spikes before
  somebody walks in.
- **Legendary: *Thorn Whip*** on whoever is closest, twice if the first one misses. The
  10-foot pull drags the target through the spikes: 2d4 per 5 feet.
- **He attacks nobody in round one.** If they back off the tree here, he stops.

## Round two

- **Action: *Erupting Earth*** on the two closest. 3d12, Dexterity save at DC 16 for half,
  and the ground it hits stays difficult. The reason it is this spell and not *Call
  Lightning* is that it costs no concentration and the spikes stay up.
- **Legendary: Step** to a statue thirty feet off, then ***Thorn Whip*** from there. The
  character who closed to melee is left in the spike growth with nothing in reach.
- **If Aniess has gone invisible or hidden:** ***Faerie Fire*** instead, on the 20-foot
  cube she was last in.

## Round three — when somebody reaches him

If Pax crosses the spikes in one move, he takes the damage and reaches him.

- **Two of them in melee: *Stoneskin*.** Their weapons are nonmagical, so everything they
  land is halved from here, and the halved damage lowers the DC of his concentration
  saves. He drops *Spike Growth* to cast it.
- **Three or more, or the party split: *Wall of Stone*** instead — ten panels across the
  hilltop, cutting the melee off from the casters, and he ends up on the side with fewer
  of them.
- **Bonus action: *Healing Word*** on himself whenever he is under half, on a turn he
  casts nothing else levelled.

## Round four

- **Action: *The Ancestor*.** He stands up Large with 45 temporary hit points and swings
  twice a turn at 1d12 + 5, reach 10 feet. Play him as the same man, larger.
- From here he is trying to hurt them. The 45 temporary hit points keep him up for the
  rest of the fight.

## Round five and after

- ***Call Lightning***, once a round, at whoever is furthest from the others.
- ***Hold Person*** on Jasper.
- ***Meld into Stone*** into the rock of the hilltop when three of them have him
  surrounded. Nothing reaches him in there, and it costs him his next turn to step back
  out. The statues are too small to hold him.

## If they do go for the tree

- ***Wall of Stone*** in a ring around the trunk, on the turn the first axe lands. Ten
  feet of stone, and nobody can reach the trunk.
- After that he stops defending himself: no *Stoneskin*, no stepping away, straight to the
  Ancestor and the nearest body.

## How it ends

**He stops fighting if:**

- They step away from the tree and put the axe down. He does not pursue, and the water and
  the garden are still on offer.
- Anybody mends the tree, waters it, or puts a seed in the soil in front of him. That ends
  the fight.
- They get him to half hit points and then stop. He stops too — nobody has landed on this
  island in his lifetime.

**At 0 hit points** he is dying, not dead — stabilise him and he lives, and either way he
gets his last words out. The rest of the session is unchanged: the bell, the hall, the
island going under, the dragon coming out of the water
([the island of the thinkers](island-of-the-thinkers.md), Under the Hill).

## Dials

- **Too hard:** 90 hit points, one Legendary Resistance, no *Stoneskin*, and skip the
  Ancestor.
- **Too easy:** three legendary actions instead of two, keep the storm overhead for 4d10
  *Call Lightning*, and open with *Stoneskin* already up.
- **Five against one is five turns to his one.** The two legendary actions and the
  reaction are what let him fight five characters; if David forgets them for a round, hand
  them back.
