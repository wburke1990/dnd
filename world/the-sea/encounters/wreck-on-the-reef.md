# The Wreck on the Reef

An open-water encounter, built for [Blackacre](../../../characters/blackacre.md) out of
Sam's 8/17 chat. Sayid is not in it. It has a wreck to dive, merfolk holding it, and a
courier's satchel with Sayid's writ in it — Blackacre's birth name and his sea-name on
one sheet of paper, with a price under them.

Run it on any day of open water. It fits the **Maalm → Brauron** leg in place of a
[sea-table roll](../bestiary/sea-beasts.md), before the storm that carries the party to
the [Thinkers](../../suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/island-of-the-thinkers.md) —
see the [cold-run packet](../../suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/cold-run-taverns-to-thinkers.md) §3.
Runs in about ninety minutes. Uses the shipwreck-and-merfolk maps.

---

## What happened, this morning

- A **Company hulk** was carrying **mast-timber** down the coast out of Musleheim —
  deck cargo of spars, six casks of pitch in the hold, twenty-two crew.
- A **raider of Sayid's tribes** took her at first light. The fleets cannot grow
  timber and cannot buy it in quantity, so they take it
  ([floating fleet](../lore/floating-fleet.md) — *why a druid is gold*).
- The pitch caught during the boarding. Both ships burned to the waterline and went
  down together on a coral shelf, the raider across the hulk's stern, in **twenty-five
  feet of water**. Two mast-tops stand out of the sea. A raft of burning spars is still
  on the surface and drifting south onto the kelp.
- **Sixty-one dead.** The merfolk of the reef have been cutting them out of the rigging
  since dawn and stripping their metal.
- **Two people are alive.** **Goodcatch**, a dragonborn druid of the raider's crew,
  pinned under a deck beam in an air pocket in the hulk's hold. And a halfling deckhand
  who swam clear and is on the reef-top a quarter-mile off, who will not come back into
  the water and tells the party nothing Goodcatch does not also tell them.
- **The courier** died in the raider's stern cabin with his satchel still clipped to
  him. The merfolk have him hung in the kelp with the rest of the metal.

## What Sayid is doing `[DM ruling — open]`

He holds **four tribes** of the Floating Fleet, not the whole of it, and he is pushing
for the fleet and the mainland to come back together. His four tribes are majority
halfling, as the fleet is, and he speaks for all the races in them. Blackacre's own rule
covers dragonborn only.

After the mutiny, half the tribes went home and he kept four. He put out the writ so
those four could see him working to bring back the man who left.

> ⚠️ Sam owns fleet lore ([floating fleet](../lore/floating-fleet.md)). Any of this is
> his to overrule.

## What the encounter settles, and what it does not

Blackacre learns he is wanted by name, alive for a price and dead for half of it, on
paper that is in every port. He learns Sayid held four tribes and is taking timber. He
does not learn where Sayid is, and does not meet him.

---

## 1 · The smoke

> Smoke stands off the water two miles ahead, black and low, bending south with the
> wind. There is no land under it.

A watch check (DC by weather) reads it early enough to choose the approach. Failure
brings them into the slick before they see the mast-tops.

## 2 · The surface

> Fire is lying on the water in a sheet thirty yards across, burning low and yellow.
> Inside it and around it there is timber — spars, deck planking, a hatch cover riding
> up and down. Two mast-tops stand out of the sea, one leaning on the other, rigging
> still strung between them, and there are bodies in the rigging.
>
> Under the surface the water is clear all the way to the coral. Two hulls are down
> there, one lying across the other.
>
> Sharks are working the bodies that have floated free. Between the sharks and the
> wreck there are people swimming. Waists and up they are men and women. Below that
> they are fish. They carry knives and they are cutting the dead out of the ropes.

**Six reef sharks** (CR 1/2) are feeding. They do not come at a swimmer while the
merfolk are driving them, and they come at anything bleeding when the merfolk stop.

## 3 · Kymo, and what she wants

**Kymo** speaks for the reef — grey-scaled from the waist down, a fisherman's knife in
her hand, an iron ring on a cord that came off one of the dead an hour ago. She talks
in trade-pidgin and she is not frightened of the boat.

The reef is hers, the wreck is on it, and both are burning. She will not let anything
be taken off it until three things are done, and she says all three before anything
else:

1. **The fire.** The burning raft of spars is drifting onto the shallow kelp south of
   the wreck. Get it off, or put it out.
2. **The pitch.** Six casks in the hulk's hold, three of them split already. Stopper
   the rest before they go.
3. **The dead's metal stays hers.** Everything else on the wreck they can have,
   including the man in the stern cabin and the satchel on him.

While the party work, the merfolk keep the sharks off. If the party refuse her, or take
metal, the merfolk pull back to the kelp and stop driving.

**The work.**

| The job | How it goes |
|---|---|
| Drag the burning raft clear | DC 13 STR (Athletics), lines off the sloop, three rounds; a failure strikes a hull box from the fire |
| Douse it | Create or Destroy Water covers it in two castings; Shape Water parts it a section at a time |
| Stopper a cask | one round each, DC 12 — Mending, carpenter's tools, or a shaped plug of green wood |
| Growing the kelp back | not required; Kymo thanks them for it |

Pay her and she gives up the courier, the satchel, and the run of both hulls. She also
tells them what she saw: the raider came out of the north at first light, and the fire
started aboard the big ship, not the small one.

## 4 · Under

**Rules to have ready.** Swim speed is half. Holding breath is 1 + CON modifier minutes,
minimum thirty seconds; a check while out of breath is at disadvantage and then the
character starts drowning. Ranged attacks underwater are at disadvantage past normal
range, melee at disadvantage unless the weapon stabs. Fire does nothing down here.
**Shape Water** and **Water Breathing**, if Blackacre has them, let the party work below
without the breath rules.

**The two hulls, for the map:**

- **The raider, on top** — burned to the waterline, lying across the hulk's stern.
  Open deck, no hatches left. **The stern cabin** is intact and full of water: a table
  bolted down, a locker, and the courier still in his seat with the satchel clipped to
  him. Two feet of the cabin roof holds air.
- **The hulk, underneath** — deck cargo mostly gone or floating. **The main hatch** is
  open and the hold is enterable. Inside: the pitch casks, cordage, four dead, and a
  fallen deck beam. Under the beam is Goodcatch, in an air pocket about a foot deep
  against the sole of the deck above.
- **The drop-off** is forty yards south. The coral falls away and the water goes from
  clear green to nothing.

**Finding her.** Anyone in the hold hears knocking — a marlinspike on a plank, three
and three. Blackacre's animals, Sarric's tracking, or a straight Perception check get
there faster.

**Freeing her.** The beam is fouled in cordage and pinned by cargo above it.

- DC 15 STR (Athletics) to lift, or **two characters working it together at DC 12
  each** in the same round.
- Cutting the cordage first (one round, DC 10) drops it to DC 12 / DC 10.
- On a failed check by 5 or more the cargo above shifts: the air pocket loses half its depth
  and the rounds remaining halve.
- **Rounds remaining.** The hold is settling. **Six rounds** from the knocking to the pocket
  going under. After that she is holding her breath with the rest of them, and she is
  spent — she does not swim herself out.

**Blood.** Any creature that takes damage in the water brings sharks the next round
unless the merfolk are driving. If the party keep bleeding into the water, something
comes up from the drop-off and stops at the limit of visibility. It is as long as the
hulk. At level 3 the party cannot fight it. End the dive.

## 5 · Goodcatch

A dragonborn druid, brown-scaled, forty-ish, one arm broken. **She is Amatssou**, out of
Tripoca, and she goes under a sea-name for the same reason he does. She has been under a
beam since dawn and she is not going to fight anyone.

She reads the name off him the moment he gives it, and she says so:

> "Blackacre. That's paper in every port from here to Mukka."

Her lines, as they come up:

- **On why she is aboard.** *"They came for me two seasons after you left. There
  aren't many of us. Somebody has to keep the hulls."*
- **On the mutiny.** *"Half the tribes went home. He held four. He says he held them
  because he was right."*
- **On the writ.** *"He wants you brought in on your own feet. He'll take you the other
  way. He's put money on both."*
- **On the night he walked** `[DM — invented; Sam can correct the count and the dead]`:
  *"Eleven people died on that deck the night you walked off it. None of them were
  dragonborn. That's the part you could live with."*
- **On the timber.** *"You can't grow a fleet out of fish. He takes wood. What did you
  think he was going to do?"*

**What she does after.** She is obliged to report that she saw him, and she will say
so plainly if asked. Whether she reports depends on how they handle her. She can be
carried to the next port, left on the reef-top with the halfling, or killed in the
water where nobody would ever know.

Blackacre will not spill dragonborn blood, and Goodcatch is dragonborn and one of
Sayid's. Put her in the boat and let the party decide what to do with her. This is one
of the smaller versions of the endgame choice called for in
[the DM notes](../../../characters/blackacre-dm-notes.md).

## 6 · The satchel

The courier is a halfling in fleet cloth with a Company-made writing case. The satchel
holds **nine copies of the same document**, wrapped in oilcloth and dry, each addressed
to a different harbour — Mukka, Aar, Aal, and six more. Hand Sam the printed sheet:
**[Sayid's writ](../../../handouts/sayids-writ.md)**. Nine copies for nine harbours: the
document is circulating, and this one never reached the port it was addressed to.

Also in the cabin: forty-one gold in mixed coin, a chart of the coast with the timber
route marked in three different inks, and a sealed second packet addressed to a name
nobody at the table knows yet — leave it sealed if they leave it sealed.

## 7 · Off the wreck

- **Timber.** The hulk's deck cargo is mast-stock. Blackacre can cut a **spare spar**
  out of it, and the sloop can carry one lashed along the rail. If the storm off Brauron
  breaks his mast, he has it.
- **Salvage.** Cordage, a hatch cover, sailcloth, four barrels of stores. Enough to
  clear two hull boxes without a port.
- **The dead.** Sixty-one of them, in the rigging and on the coral. There is no burying
  them. If anyone keeps a watch over the water before they sail, that is
  [the Wandering](../lore/wandering-dragonborn.md) — water, a coin, a verse, and go.
