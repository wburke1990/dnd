# Olmec Platform and the Were-Jaguars `[stub]`

An Olmec-style earthen platform stands in the jungle. Were-jaguars live on it.
The party can descend through a cave into the abyss below.

## Contents

- **The platform.** A raised earthen mound with stepped terraces. Basalt
  heads, half-buried, stand in the jungle around it and give cover.
- **The Olmec Dragon.** A crocodile-like creature with flame-shaped eyebrows,
  a bulbous nose, a forked tongue and fangs shaped like an upside-down U.
  Carvings of it cover the platform, and the party may meet it.
- **The shaman kings.** The shaman king's rank passes from parent to child.
  The shaman kings turn into jaguars. They bury their dead in the platform.
  When the Milky Way stands over the cave, they go in and come out as
  jaguars.
- **The mountain cave.** The shaman kings live in a cave in a mountain beside
  the platform. The cave leads down to the underworld, and its mouth is
  carved as the Dragon's jaws.
- **Were-jaguars.** The current shaman king becomes a jaguar at night. Jaguar
  cubs guard the cave. Carvings of baby-faced figures with a cleft head and a
  snarling mouth cover the cave walls. The were-jaguars hunt from the
  terraces.
- **Ball court.** A sunken court with sloped walls and a heavy rubber ball
  that damages anyone it hits. The were-jaguars may offer a game instead of
  a fight.
- **Jade and mirrors.** Buried jade offerings and polished hematite mirrors.
  A creature that looks into a mirror sees a jaguar's face.
- **Terrain.** Mist, vines, a swamp and a slow river fill the jungle.
- **Peaceful passage.** The shaman king lets the party pass if they perform a
  ritual or pay a debt.

## The Milky Way

The platform is laid out along the Milky Way. The dead travel along it to the
underworld.

- **Alignment.** The platform, the ball court and the mountain cave lie on
  one line, running toward the point where the Milky Way meets the horizon.
- **The cave.** On clear nights the Milky Way stands over the cave mouth.
  Souls of the dead enter the cave and descend.
- **Carvings.** A band of carved dots circles each terrace and ends at the
  cave.

## The three levels

The universe has three levels: the abyss below, the material world, and the
heavens above.

- **The abyss.** The underworld, below the earth and full of water. Caves
  lead down to it, and the dead go down to it.
- **The material world.** The living world. Carvings of the Olmec Dragon
  cover the platform, which stands on the surface of the earth.
- **The heavens.** The sky above. The Milky Way crosses it, and the sun, moon
  and stars move through it.
- **The center.** The Maize God stands at the center. The mountain, the cave
  and the Milky Way above it lie on one vertical line.

## The descent

The party goes down through the cave into the abyss.

- **The way down.** The cave passage ends at flooded chambers. The passage
  continues under water.
- **Demons.** Demons live in the abyss, ruled by the Death God.
- **The dead.** The shaman kings' ancestors are in the abyss, and they can
  be spoken to.

## The gods in D&D terms

Each god uses the stat block listed here. `[OPEN]` These choices can change.

- **Olmec Dragon.** An ancient black dragon, a swamp dragon with a
  crocodile's body.
- **Bird Monster.** A roc, with a harpy eagle's crest.
- **Maize God.** A celestial. Maize cobs sprout from a cleft in the top of its
  head.
- **Rain Spirit.** A storm giant or an air elemental, with were-jaguar
  features. It is the Maize God in seed form.
- **Banded-eye God.** A fey, drawn in profile. It is another form of the
  Maize God.
- **Feathered Serpent.** A couatl, a serpent covered in feathers.
- **Fish Monster.** A sahuagin baron with a shark's teeth and a fish's face.
- **Death God.** A demon lord with a fleshless jaw. It rules the abyss.
