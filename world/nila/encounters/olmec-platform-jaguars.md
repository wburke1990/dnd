# Olmec Platform and the Were-Jaguars `[stub]`

> **Idea only.** Placed nowhere on the map yet.

The encounter uses an Olmec-style earthen platform in the jungle. Were-jaguars
live on it.

## Ideas to include

- **The platform.** A raised earthen mound with stepped terraces. Colossal
  basalt heads sit half-buried in the jungle floor around it and serve as
  cover and landmarks.
- **Were-jaguars.** A priest-king who becomes a jaguar at night, and a few
  guardians in jaguar-cub form. Olmec "were-jaguar baby" carvings (a
  snarling infant with a cleft head) mark their lair.
- **Ball court.** A sunken court with sloped walls and a heavy rubber ball
  that damages anyone it hits. The were-jaguars may demand a game in place
  of a fight.
- **Jade and mirrors.** Buried jade offerings and polished hematite mirrors.
  A creature looking into a mirror sees itself as a jaguar.
- **Jungle terrain.** Mist, vines and a flooded cenote. The were-jaguars
  hunt from the terraces.
- **Avoiding the fight.** The priest-king accepts a ritual the party learns
  or a debt the party pays. Ritual and debt still to be decided.
