# Olmec Platform and the Were-Jaguars `[stub]`

An Olmec-style earthen platform stands in the jungle. Were-jaguars live on it.

## Ideas to include

- **The platform.** A raised earthen mound with stepped terraces. Basalt
  heads, half-buried, stand in the jungle around it and give cover.
- **The Olmec Dragon.** A crocodile-like creature with a cleft head,
  flame-shaped eyebrows and a downturned mouth. Carvings of it cover the
  platform, and the party may meet it.
- **The shaman kings.** The shaman kings pass the title down a family line
  and turn into jaguars. Their dead are buried in the platform.
- **The mountain cave.** The shaman kings live in a cave in a mountain beside
  the platform. The cave is a portal to the underworld, and its mouth is
  carved as the Dragon's jaws.
- **Were-jaguars.** The priest-king becomes a jaguar at night. A few jaguar
  cubs guard the cave. Carvings of a snarling infant with a cleft head cover
  its walls.
- **Ball court.** A sunken court with sloped walls and a heavy rubber ball
  that damages anyone it hits. The were-jaguars may offer a game instead of
  a fight.
- **Jade and mirrors.** Buried jade offerings and polished hematite mirrors.
  A creature that looks into a mirror sees a jaguar instead of its own face.
- **Jungle terrain.** The jungle has mist, vines and a flooded cenote. The
  were-jaguars hunt from the terraces.
- **Avoiding the fight.** The priest-king lets the party pass if they perform
  a ritual or pay a debt.

## The pantheon

Carvings of these gods stand on the terraces and around the cave mouth.

- **Maize God.** Maize cobs sprout from a cleft in the top of its head.
- **Rain Spirit.** Has were-jaguar features. Karl Taube reads it as the
  Maize God as a seed.
- **Banded-eye God.** Shown only in profile. Taube reads it as another form
  of the Maize God.
- **Feathered Serpent.** A serpent covered in feathers.
- **Fish Monster.** A creature with a fish's face and body.
- **Water God.** Some scholars see him as the earlier form of Tlaloc and
  Chac.
- **Death God.** Shown with a fleshless jaw.
