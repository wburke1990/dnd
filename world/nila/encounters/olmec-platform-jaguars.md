# Olmec Platform and the Were-Jaguars `[stub]`

An Olmec-style earthen platform stands in the jungle. Were-jaguars live on it.

## Contents

- **The platform.** A raised earthen mound with stepped terraces. Basalt
  heads, half-buried, stand in the jungle around it and give cover.
- **The Olmec Dragon.** A crocodile-like creature with a cleft head,
  flame-shaped eyebrows and a downturned mouth. Carvings of it cover the
  platform, and the party may meet it.
- **The shaman kings.** The title passes from parent to child. The shaman
  kings turn into jaguars, and their dead are buried in the platform.
- **The mountain cave.** The shaman kings live in a cave in a mountain beside
  the platform. The cave is a portal to the underworld, and its mouth is
  carved as the Dragon's jaws.
- **Were-jaguars.** The current shaman king becomes a jaguar at night. Jaguar
  cubs guard the cave. Carvings of a snarling infant with a cleft head cover
  the cave walls.
- **Ball court.** A sunken court with sloped walls and a heavy rubber ball
  that damages anyone it hits. The were-jaguars may offer a game instead of
  a fight.
- **Jade and mirrors.** Buried jade offerings and polished hematite mirrors.
  A creature that looks into a mirror sees a jaguar's face.
- **Jungle terrain.** The jungle has mist, vines and a flooded cenote. The
  were-jaguars hunt from the terraces.
- **Passing without a fight.** The shaman king lets the party pass if they
  perform a ritual or pay a debt.

## The Milky Way

Many peoples of Mesoamerica and North America believed the dead travel to the
afterlife along the Milky Way. Some researchers propose that settlements were
laid out along the Milky Way, and that equinox alignments came later.

- **Alignment.** The platform, the ball court and the mountain cave lie on
  one line, running toward the point where the Milky Way meets the horizon.
- **Souls.** On clear nights the Milky Way stands over the cave mouth. Souls
  of the dead enter the cave and descend to the underworld.
- **The shaman kings.** They go into the cave when the Milky Way stands over
  it and come out as jaguars.
- **Carvings.** A band of carved dots circles each terrace and ends at the
  cave.

## The three tiers

The Olmec and later Mesoamerican peoples held the universe to have three
tiers, joined by a central axis.

- **The abyss.** The underworld, below the earth and full of water. Caves
  are its doors. The dead go down to it.
- **The material world.** The living world. Carvings of the Olmec Dragon
  represent the surface of the earth, and the platform stands on it.
- **The heavens.** The sky above. The Milky Way crosses it and the sun, moon
  and stars move through it.
- **The axis.** The Maize God stands at the center of the three tiers. The
  mountain, the cave and the Milky Way over it make that line at the site.
- **Passing between tiers.** The shaman kings go down through the cave to the
  abyss and come back as jaguars.

## The pantheon

Carvings of these gods stand on the terraces and around the cave mouth.

- **Maize God.** Maize cobs sprout from a cleft in the top of its head.
- **Rain Spirit.** It has were-jaguar features. Karl Taube identifies it as
  the Maize God in seed form.
- **Banded-eye God.** It is shown in profile. Taube identifies it as another
  form of the Maize God.
- **Feathered Serpent.** A serpent covered in feathers.
- **Fish Monster.** A creature with a fish's face and body.
- **Water God.** Some scholars identify it as the earlier form of Tlaloc and
  Chac.
- **Death God.** It has a fleshless jaw.
