# The Market Mushrooms

> **Pax bought these in [session 12](../../../sessions/session-12.md)**, from a trader in
> the Aar market, purple spots on the cap and orange gills, and Andrew means to eat them.
> Run this whenever he does. It takes about fifteen minutes of play, split across the
> in-game night.
>
> **Pax is told his true name in *Eight*.** Read *Eight* and *The name* before running it.

---

## What they are

Purple-spotted cap, orange gills. A supply of them, bought under a standing deal with the
trader, so Pax can do this more than once.

**They are not the mushroom from under the elm.** The one Brother Reedhand told him not
to eat was white with blue flecks that moved
([Pax's four-day vision](../../../characters/pax.md)). These are a different thing bought
off a stall. Pax has not said he knows the difference.

They are a real toxin and a strong hallucinogen. One dose will not kill him.

---

## The rulings

### The check is Constitution

**DC 13 Constitution saving throw** when he eats one. It is a poison going into a body,
so it is CON, the same as any other ingested toxin. It is not Wisdom; the mushroom has no
mind.

### Pax may decline the save

Say yes when he asks — he bought these on purpose. Declining is not the same as rolling
badly, and it costs more:

| | What he gets | Duration | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Save succeeds** | An hour of colour, and a headache. No visions | 1 hour | None |
| **Save fails** | Visions one to seven | 8 hours | 1 level of exhaustion |
| **He declines the save** | Visions one to eight | 12 hours | 2 levels of exhaustion |

**He is told the name only if he declines the save.**

**Eating more than one does not take him further.** It adds exhaustion and nothing else.
Tell him plainly if he asks.

---

## Two kinds of vision

Pax is the child of a Celestial and a nameless earth-person
([lineages and afterlives](../lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md) §6). **The earth visions
and the star visions are separate. Do not blend them.** Run them alternating, one at a
time, and let the rest of the table do other things in between. Read them flat.

- **The earth visions are the firbolg dead.** He is inside people who are already dead, one
  after another, and not one of them has a name. These are the same earth dreams from his
  mother's side ([Pax's DM notes](../../../characters/pax-dm-notes.md)), which Pax has
  always credited to the Green Revelation.
- **The star visions are the celestial side**, in the register of the four-day vision.
  There he is one person, and he is named.

**The earth visions give him no name. The star visions give him his name, and it is read
off a list.**

## The visions

### One · Twenty minutes in — earth

> You are standing on a shore. You are a woman, you are eight feet tall, and your hands
> are not your hands.
>
> Above the harbour a dragonborn is cutting a standing rock with a chisel. He has been at
> it a long time. There are gnomes on the beach behind him and more of them coming out of
> the boats.
>
> Nobody on this shore has a name. You know it without being told.

`[DM]` **That is [Musa](../../suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/lore/the-fallen-gnome-kingdom.md)
cutting the watch-stone**, and the party read its inscriptions at Aar this session. Do not
say so. If Pax describes it to them afterward, let Sarric or Aniess make the connection.

### Two · Forty minutes in — star

> Threads of light run out of every living thing and along the ground. You have seen these
> before, from the stars.
>
> From here they are a foot off the ground and your hand goes through them without
> catching.
>
> Every thread in reach runs to Sarric, or to Jasper, or to Aniess. None of them run to
> you.

### Three · An hour in — earth

> You are a man in a treeline. A dragonborn comes through it and he is bigger than you.
>
> He puts a sword into your belly and holds it there. You go down onto him and the two of
> you are on the ground together for a while.
>
> Then you are on your back, and he is standing up, and he is wiping it.

### Four · Two hours in — star

> The ground goes out from under you and you fall upward. You have done this before.
>
> You rise until the world is small below you. It is flat. You can see all of it at once,
> and you can see the edge.
>
> You do not reach the stars. You stop under them and hang there.

`[DM]` **This confirms the flat earth**, and it is a hallucination showing him what he
already believes (`[Andrew, session 11]`). Do not treat it as evidence and do not have
anything in the world contradict it during the vision. Later, the horizon and the atlas
can contradict him.

### Five · Three hours in — earth

> You are a child. You are running over wet ground and the ground is holding your feet.
>
> Behind you there are gnomes in plate. They are not running, because they do not have to.
>
> You are still running when it stops.

### Six · Four to six hours in — star

> Something is moving above you, between you and the stars. It is large enough that the
> stars go out where it passes and come back behind it.
>
> You turn toward it. There is darkness and distant light.
>
> It does not speak. It goes on doing what it is doing, and it does not turn toward you.

### Seven · Seven hours in — earth

> You are in the ground and you have been for a long time. There are others in the ground
> on either side of you.
>
> Something is cutting down through the rock above you. It has been getting closer for a
> while, and there is a great deal of it.
>
> Everyone down here has noticed it.

`[DM]` **This is [Haals](../../suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-haals-runoff.md)**,
where the mine is cutting into the firbolg graves, and the party are headed that way. Pax
and Sarric dream of it more often the closer they get. If you run this before Haals, Pax
has already seen the mine from below.

### Eight · Ten hours in, only if he declined the save

> You hear your name. This time it is said to you.

**Say it out loud at the table: *Auralith Vael'Tharos*.** It is the name on Andrew's own
sheet, and Pax has not been able to hold it since the four-day vision. He holds it now.

> Then you hear it again, somewhere else, in a different voice. It is being read off a
> list. There is a pause after it, and then another name you do not know, said the same
> way. Then a third.

## The name  `[settled 9/1]`

**The forgotten name from the four-day vision is Auralith Vael'Tharos**, which settles the
open question in [Pax's DM notes](../../../characters/pax-dm-notes.md). It is Andrew's own
invention. We use it, and this session tells him where it came from.

**Knowing the name gives him no new ability. It gives someone else a way to bind him.** A
true name lets the holder command or bind the one it belongs to
([campaign overview](../lore/campaign-overview.md)), and Planar Binding and its kin succeed
outright against a named target. What Pax has after this night:

- He knows his own name and can say it.
- He knows **somebody else has it written down**, alongside names he does not know.
- He does not know who, or what the list is for.

`[OPEN]` **Whose list.** The party harvest names off tombs for Preem, and there are
[other crews doing the same](../../suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md).
Preem is the obvious answer. Leave it unnamed for now.

## What the party sees

> He sat down some hours ago and he has not got up. His eyes are open. He answers when
> you talk to him and the answers are about something else.
>
> Twice now he has said "not to me."

No convulsions and no seizure. This is not the four-day vision, and Reedhand is not
present.

**Have Sarric in the room for the earth visions if the table allows it.** Pax is describing
Sarric's own dead, and Sarric knows what the firbolg dead are.

---

## Afterward

- He wakes at 8 hours, or 12 if he declined the save, with the exhaustion in the table
  above and no lasting damage.
- **He remembers all of it.** The name stays this time.
- **Doing it again gives him the same seven visions.** It stops at seven, and the name is
  not said twice.
