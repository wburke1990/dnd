# PART ONE — BACKGROUND

## Why they are let in

The zealots are an island remnant. They have held the caldera 1,900 years behind cliffs
and one guarded channel, their gods have stopped coming
([Lonka / Jotunheim](../lore/lonka-jotunheim.md) §5), and there are not enough of them.
Two needs follow, and both work in the party's favour at the gate:

- **More dragonborn.** Outside blood, and any dragonborn is worth having. A wandering
  nomad is of the schismatic branch, but he is dragonborn, and they need him more than
  they mind.
- **Converts.** `[settled — Will 8/16]` They mean to spread the creed beyond the island
  as well as guard it. This is new about them and it points at the fleet (§6). The
  party is the first audience to walk in for years.

So everything is shown to them, and shown proudly. The teaching at the trees is put on
for guests. The party is shown everything in
[Askr and Embla](../lore/askr-and-embla.md) openly.

**What hospitality costs.** The party learns all of it as guests. The zealots have fed
and housed them.

## Blackacre, the trials, and the bride

`[settled — Will 8/16]` He is offered a **bride** on completion of the **coming-of-age
trials** — the rite every zealot passes into adulthood by.

What it carries:

- **It is a marriage into the creed.** The trials end at the two trees, where the
  creation is taught and the vows are sworn. He cannot take her without standing where
  Askr and Embla stand and saying what is said there.
- **It ends the zealot/nomad rift on the zealots' terms.** His arc is to mend that rift
  ([Blackacre](../characters/blackacre.md)). Under this offer he would join the
  zealots. `[OPEN]` whether anyone at the table says so, or whether it goes unsaid.
- **It is a smaller version of the fleet offer.** At the end of his arc they ask him to lead
  the fleet, and the sheet already says to give him practice at smaller versions first.
  This is the practice: a real thing he wants, offered on their terms.
- `[OPEN]` who she is, whether she chose it, and whether she believes the creation. A
  bride who has doubts of her own is a different scene from one who has none.

## The rakshasas

`[settled — Will 8/16]` The demons who helped the zealots reach the island are **still
here**, in a temple inside the caldera, ~1,900 years later. This answers two open
questions: the road under the sea **was** demon-opened
([Lonka / Jotunheim](../lore/lonka-jotunheim.md) §4), and the rakshasas of the Ravana
material are **in the present caldera** as well as in its legends.

**What they are.** Shape-shifting, man-eating, out of the Ramayana. Demons are
true-named and do not age or die of old age
([lineages & afterlives](../lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md) §3), and they are not
descended from Taniyn. The zealots' claim to this island rests on an alliance with
demons, who are not their god's kin.

**They are venerated, and the faith has changed around them.** `[settled — Will 8/16]`
The zealots honour them as the ancient allies of the reconquest, and they have been
present for every generation of it. The priests who taught the creation at the trees
are all dead. The rakshasas are alive, and they are the only surviving witnesses to
what was done here.

**They do not direct anything, and never have.** No demon has ever stood up in the
caldera and told the zealots what to believe. They are asked — how was it done before,
what did the old rite say, was there more to it — and they answer, and the answer is a
little more than was asked for. A generation later that answer is the practice, and the
one who gave it is still there to be asked about the next thing. Over 1,900 years the
rites have taken more blood, each addition small enough that nobody living saw a change.
`[for the table]` Never let a rakshasa argue for anything. They remember, and they are
asked, and that is the whole of what they do.

That also answers the open question of who supplied the creation at the trees
([Askr and Embla](../lore/askr-and-embla.md)): nobody had to invent it all at once.
They were present when the two trees were named and they have been consulted about it
ever since.

`[for the table]` The party cannot expose them. The zealots love them.

**Who they are actually up against.** Not the party.
**[Duha al-Warraq](../characters/duha-al-warraq.md)** keeps the books here and has spent
her life arguing that the fleet should sail to the dragons rather than against the
island. Every time she puts it, someone asks the rakshasas, and the answer comes back a
little longer than the question and ends in blood. She has lost that argument a dozen
times and does not know why. She reveres them like everyone else, and they have never
said her name.

**What they know.** They travelled with the zealots. They saw two trees carried under
the sea, planted on the wall, and named. They know what the ash and the elm are, and
who wrote the creation, and when.

**Why they say very little.** Reasons:

- They answer only what is literally asked. A vague question gets a true answer that
  tells the asker nothing.
- Nobody would believe them anyway. A demon's word against the zealot priesthood is
  worth nothing inside the caldera, and the rakshasas know it. They can sell what they
  know, but cannot use it against the zealots.
- The zealots' doctrine is nothing to them. Whether the two trees are dragon-children
  or stolen forest-dead does not matter to a demon.
- They would rather sell it. `[proposed]` the price is a name — the same trade in names
  Preem deals in ([the name quest](../lore/demons-and-the-hotspot.md)), with the demons
  as buyers rather than sellers. These are old demons: they guard their own names and
  buy other people's.

**What the party can get from them.** The one living account of the two trees being
planted, from witnesses. The zealots would not accept it as proof.

**What they took for the road.** `[settled — Will 8/16]` **Nothing.** They opened the
road and then came through it with the zealots and joined the massacre, and that was
the payment — they wanted to be in it. Nothing was owed before and nothing has been
owed since. The party will find no ongoing payment or debt.

So the alliance has never cost the zealots anything, and they have never had to answer
for it. Demons took part in the killing whose blood the two trees were consecrated in.

`[proposed]` **Why they are still here 1,900 years later.** Because there will be
another one. They are not garrisoned or owed anything; they are waiting for the next
massacre. The zealots mean to sail to Moku and do at that island what they did here
([Lonka / Jotunheim](../lore/lonka-jotunheim.md) §6), so the rakshasas sail with the
fleet. This gives them a part in Blackacre's final arc using what is already
established.

`[OPEN]` whether any of them is wearing a dragonborn face in the caldera now. They are
shape-shifters, so any of them could be doing it.

# PART TWO — RUN SHEET

## The welcome

`[to build]` The channel, the Dragon Turtle let off its watch, the landing, the housing,
the meal. Play the hospitality straight and warm. They want visitors to stay.

## Offers to each PC

Guests get offers. One each:

- **Blackacre** — the trials and the bride (above).
- **Pax** — the trees. He is walked up to them like everyone else and taught the
  creation. See [the rite](jotunheim-trees-rite.md).
- **Jasper** — the books. The zealots' own liturgical book is in a library here, and
  **[Duha al-Warraq](../characters/duha-al-warraq.md)**, who keeps it, is glad of a
  guest who can read. Nearly everything the party needs comes through her hands.
- **Aniess** — `[OPEN]`.
- **Sarric** — `[OPEN]`.

## The teaching at the trees

Run it as a public ceremony for guests: the whole settlement, the children answering, the
priest reciting. Everything in
[Askr and Embla, *What the party sees*](../lore/askr-and-embla.md) is available here
without a single roll — the priest says *found* and *begotten* in the same hour, in
front of them, at the top of the wall, with the ash under the roots ten feet away.

## The temple in the grove

`[to build]` The rakshasa temple. `[proposed]` it stands in the **Ashoka Vatika** — the
walled grove already on file ([Lonka / Jotunheim](../lore/lonka-jotunheim.md) §4) — so
the temple stands in the Ashoka Vatika. How the party gets in, and
whether the zealots take them or they find it, is open.
