# The Game Preserve

## A D&D Encounter — Design Note `[draft]`

A hunting preserve run for profit. Clients pay to hunt the animals inside, and
to hunt people. One of the sites marked on [Preem's map](../characters/preem.md);
the dot sits inside the fence.

Real-world sources, run together: *The Most Dangerous Game* (Richard Connell —
General Zaroff keeps an island and hunts the men who wash up on it), *The Island
of Doctor Moreau* (H. G. Wells — a man on a remote island cuts beasts into the
shapes of men), and *Jurassic Park* (a preserve of dangerous animals run as a
paid attraction, behind fences that hold until they don't).

---

## Where it is

An island in the strait **north of [Raand](../lore/nila-atlas.md)** (94, 74) and
**south across the narrow strait from [Sukkek](../lore/nila-atlas.md)** (95, 61)
— the strait the atlas marks with strong currents. Approximate: **X94, Y68**.
`[OPEN]` exact placement, and whether it is one island or a stretch of held
coast.

## The dot — the great tree

Preem's map marks a dot inside the preserve, at **a single ancient tree, huge
and standing alone.** The retrieval site: the party applies the
[location stone](../characters/preem.md) at the tree and the instructions
appear. `[OPEN]` what is recovered here.

## Getting in

Three ways in, and the fee sets which one the party takes.

- **Pay to hunt.** A client buys entry as a hunter — **5,000 gold.** The party
  will not have it.
- **Sign up to be hunted.** The way in the party can afford, short of sneaking:
  put your name down as the quarry. You are released into the preserve and the
  paying hunters come after you. This is the non-sneaky route to the tree.
- **Sneak in** past the fence and the currents.
