# Session 09 — The Dream Quest: The Fire, the Hall, and the Weighing
## Gate Five through waking — the party's confessions, Ghassan's confession, and Ammit at the scales

> Logged from a table recap. Picks up mid-dream from
> [Session 8](session-08.md), which stopped at the offering bowl. Ran close to
> the prep — [The Dream Quest](../encounters/the-dream-quest.md), resuming at
> **Gate Five.** This closes the rite.

---

# WHAT HAPPENED

## Gate Five — Isis, Nephthys, and Selket

- The party spoke for **Ghassan** before the three judges. They argued **his
  love for his children**, **his grief for the loss of his people and his
  religion** — the last man in Maalm keeping the old gods — and **his
  charitable work.** It carried. The gate opened and Ghassan passed through.

## Gate Six — the fire

- The banks ignited and the boat had to hold the channel. **Aniess got burned
  by the fire** — she took damage, and the rule held: the wound ended the spell
  for her and **she poofed out of the dream.** She woke on the floor of the
  house and was **out for the rest of the crossing.** The other four held the
  boat and went on to the Hall without her.

## The Hall of Two Truths — the party's confessions

Anubis told the four living they'd have to state a name and a sin to remain.
Each confessed:

- **Blackacre:** *"I betrayed a good captain."*
- **Pax:** *"My sin is certainty. I called my anger righteous. I hurt those who
  hurt the earth."*
- **Sarric:** *"I burned a kid to death. I didn't know the kid was in the
  tent."*
- **Jasper:** *"We burned down a village. They made a play about it."*

## Asking Anubis about their own deaths

- **Sarric asked Anubis whether this is what waits for him when he dies.** Anubis
  answered: **no.**
- **Pax asked the same.** Anubis: *"Pax, you may stand in this hall some day, or
  you may not. I do not know the future. Your path is yours to walk."*

## Ghassan's confession and the weighing

- Ghassan was brought forward. Anubis took his heart and set it on the scale
  against the feather, and Ghassan gave his confession — the binding, the
  thirty-one years, the name in his mouth, the choice he never let his wife
  make.
- **The party lingered in the room** after the confession. So **Ammit ate
  Ghassan's heart.** His heart was devoured at the scales.

## Waking, and the choice — tell the children, or not

- The four woke on the floor of the house with Aniess, dawn through the window.
  Ghassan did not come back with them.
- **The party talked out whether to tell the children the truth** — that their
  father had enslaved their mother with her true name, and that his heart was
  eaten by Ammit at the scales. They weighed the right thing to do.
- **Pax asked the children about the binding**, and it was awkward. He was
  **visibly shaking.** *"Do you know what that spell does?"* **The children
  weren't familiar with the details** — they knew the love story, not the Planar
  Binding underneath it.
- **In the end they did not tell the children what happened** at the scales.
- The al Qahtani honored them anyway: **each party member got 100 gp** and **the
  friendship of the al Qahtani.**
- Breakfast off the terraces. **Pax loved the breakfast.**

## The coffeehouse — V. drops invisibility

- The session closed at a coffeehouse. **Pax drinks coffee by eating the beans
  and drinking the boiling water.**
- **V.** was there **invisible**, and **drank Sarric's coffee.** She **dropped
  invisibility as she drank it**, revealing herself at the table.
- **Blackacre, Pax, and Jasper all failed their perception checks** — they
  didn't clock her before the reveal.
- **V. has the maps and placed the offers.** The session ended here.

# DM NOTES & OPEN THREADS

- **The rite failed — Ammit took the heart.** Ghassan did not pass the weighing;
  his heart was devoured because the party lingered rather than leaving before
  the verdict. Per the prep the living turn back before any verdict is visible
  ([the Hall](../encounters/the-dream-quest.md#the-exit)); here the table saw the
  outcome. **The children were not told**, so as far as Muntadhir and Alizayd
  know, the party went and came back — they carry the loss, the family doesn't.
  `[OPEN]` whether that stays hidden, and whether it ever comes back on the
  party that they know and the children don't.
- **Anubis's split answer is a lineage tell — mind what it commits us to.**
  Sarric asked if the Hall is what waits for him and got a flat **no**; Pax asked
  and got a **maybe** (*"you may stand in this hall some day, or you may not"*).
  That's now canon from a god's mouth: **Sarric's soul does not go to the Duat**,
  and **Pax's might.** It lines up with the campaign's lineages-and-afterlives
  design — different peoples, different roads for the dead
  ([lineages & afterlives](../lore/lineages-and-afterlives.md)). `[OPEN]` where
  Sarric's soul *does* go, if we ever answer it, and why Pax is the open case —
  worth a look at his and Sarric's backstories before committing to an answer.
- **Aniess is the one who didn't finish.** She took fire damage at Gate Six and
  woke before the Hall — so **she made no confession** and **wasn't present for
  Ghassan's judgment.** The wound rule that Session 8 left `[OPEN]` got tested
  and played as prep intended: damage ends the spell and the sleeper wakes, no
  lasting wound. That question is now **settled at the table** — wounds wake you.
- **The four confessions are worth keeping as character material.** Each is
  player-authored and worth keeping as said — Blackacre's betrayed captain, Pax's righteous
  anger, **Sarric's burned child**, **Jasper's burned village "they made a play
  about."** These are their sins on record now; lean on them later, don't
  rewrite them. `[OPEN]` whether any belong on the PC sheets (the players' call,
  per [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)) — leaving the sheets untouched until they say.
  Two of the four confessions are **fire** — Sarric and Jasper — the two the
  serpent named at Gate One.
- **The reward, as played, differed from the prep.** The built reward is
  potions and components at cost, a safe house, and each sibling's personal
  assistance once ([The Reward](../encounters/the-dream-quest.md#the-reward)).
  The table gave **100 gp each plus the al Qahtani's friendship.** The
  standing goods-at-cost bargain and the personal debts may still be in force —
  they were the pitch at the shop — so treat the shop-at-cost and the one-time
  favors as **still available** unless you decide the 100 gp settled it.
- **The small Anubis wasn't mentioned.** The prep gives the statue to the party
  only if the rite lands, and the children keep it if it goes badly — but the
  children **don't know** it went badly. `[OPEN]` whether the family handed it
  over (they'd have no reason not to) or kept it. If you want it as a standing
  prop and a link to the under-city, hand it over next time it comes up.
- **V. reappeared at the coffeehouse, and she has the maps.** She sat invisible,
  drank Sarric's coffee, and dropped invisibility to reveal herself; three of the
  party missed the perception check. She has the maps and placed the offers, and
  the session ended on it — so **next session opens at the coffeehouse table with
  V.**, whatever the offers are.
