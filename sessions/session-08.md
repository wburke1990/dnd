# WHAT HAPPENED

## The al Qahtani house — the tour and the cast

- Picking up from [Session 7](session-07.md), the party went to the
  **al Qahtani mansion** ([the children](../characters/the-children.md)) and
  were met by **Muntadhir** and **an aide of Ali's** — Ali herself did not
  receive them in person.
- **Muntadhir walked them through the house** — the tour as built. They
  **learned the rite is cast with the *Dream of the Blue Veil* spell**, cast
  it, and **fell into the dream.** They woke on the boat on the river of
  stars.

## The dream — Gate One, the serpent

- Group Wisdom save at the serpent. **Sarric and Jasper failed** their saves
  and **felt the pull toward the snake** — the serpent found something in each
  of them that didn't quite belong. Enough of the party passed, so the boat
  went through.

## Gate Two — Apep

- All-hands rowing to keep the boat moving. **Blackacre served as captain.**
  The crew worked together and **passed the gate.**
- On the **one round they failed the DC 10 checks**, **Pax** had done least to
  keep the boat moving and had to make the **Dex save** — he **passed**, so he
  **took no damage and did not wake up.**

## Gate Three — Thoth's questions

- **Jasper knew almost every question.** **Aniess kept giving wrong answers**,
  and **David played it so the boat shook each time she answered wrong.** The
  party got enough right and moved on.
- **Thoth greeted Sarric and Jasper by name** — the two who'd failed the
  serpent's save at Gate One. It creeped the players out.

## Gate Four — the offering bowl

- **Aniess went first and tried to offer a dagger** — David let her **summon**
  one — but it **wasn't meaningful to her, so the bowl didn't accept it.**
- **The others all made their offerings before Aniess got another turn, so she
  had to go last** — and the last offering is the one that has to cost the
  most.
  - **Pax** — a **stick he'd used to hit a man** from his backstory who
    wouldn't take his shoes off inside.
  - **Jasper** — a **piece of the cornerstone of his family house**, the one
    destroyed by invaders.
  - **Sarric** — a **beehive he'd been working on** to become a beekeeper.
  - **Blackacre** — **charts** that stood for his **freedom of the seas.**
- **Aniess, last:** she summoned a **mug of beer** — her **memory of a night
  out with her friends** from her backstory. **The bowl accepted the memory.**
  As it did, **she lost it: she can no longer remember that night out with her
  friends.**

The dream stopped here. **Next session picks up at Gate Five.**
