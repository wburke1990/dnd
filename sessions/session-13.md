# WHAT HAPPENED

## The party took the job

**They agreed to steal the peseshkef for Beartholomew.**
[The godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md)
is now in play.

## The opening

**Mogg was telling the story and Doruk was drunk and huffing at it**, which is the setup for the
[Doruk and Mogg fight](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-run-to-copaa.md#the-fight--doruk-and-mogg-run).
Nobody started it.

## Sarric and Doruk

**Sarric went to him, and they talked about killing people.** Doruk told him not to feel
bad about it, which is his written line.

- **Sarric gave him Fester** — his hatred of the rat man, and taking the two fingers off.
- **Doruk gave him an orc**, cleaved shoulder to navel with his axe.
- **Doruk bought the drinks.**
- **On Setha's money.** Asked, he said she pays well for services rendered, and that she
  is not in this for money.
- **Doruk asked whether Sarric works for Preem.** Sarric said no, and Doruk dropped it.
- **Sarric asked whether Doruk was going to the temple for the killing.** He said no —
  **he is on a mission he cannot talk about.** `[OPEN]` what the Writ are going to the
  Hunt for.

**Sarric was giving Setha dirty looks** the whole time, on the strength of her being rich.

## Pax and Pim

**Pax talked Pim up and they got on.** They compared hearing voices, and **Pax gave him a
mushroom.** Pax met the centipede — [Preem's third
quasit](../characters/preems-demons.md) — without knowing what it is.

**Sarric was jealous and produced bees.**

**Then the Hunt came up, and Pim asked whether the party would be hunters or hunted.**

- **It costs 5,000 gp to join the Sacred Hunt as a hunter.** `[settled at the table 9/1]`
- **The party do not have 5,000 gp**, and said so.

**Pax introduced Pim to Sarric**, and got Sarric to bring Setha over to meet him and
Aniess.

## Pax ate a mushroom

He has **twenty dried** and **six fresh** — large, purple-spotted, orange-gilled
([the market mushrooms](../world/nila/encounters/the-market-mushrooms.md)).

**He ate one dried and passed the CON save.** What he got: heightened senses, a warm
feeling in the belly, wanting to laugh, and feeling what people were thinking.

`[ruled 9/1]` **Advantage on Insight checks for an hour.**

## Aniess and Setha

**Aniess rolled a natural 20 investigating Setha.** She now knows the **small Anubis and
the peseshkef are cut from the same stone.** They put the two side by side and compared
them.

That is [the godstone dagger](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-godstone-dagger.md)
§7 happening here instead. It was written for the canal road.

**Setha asked whether Aniess is descended from the founder families of Maalm or from the
Amseh line.** Aniess said she got the statue on the dream quest, which meant nothing to
Setha, who took it for the tombs — so they talked about the tombs they had both been in.

- **Setha asked whether Aniess works for Preem. Aniess said yes.**
- **Aniess asked whether Setha was with the Brass Jackals.** Setha: *"Those amateurs?"* —
  which is where the finest-company boast came from.
- **Sefi heard it and answered her back:** *"Setha! Don't be such a jerk!"* Nobody else in
  either crew talks to Renk that way, and Renk had nothing to say to it.
- **Setha told her the Writ are the best crew there is** and found the most names.
- **Aniess tried to tell her about the dream quest** and did not impress her.
- **Aniess tried to get Setha into a drinking contest with Jasper** and failed the
  persuasion check.

**Off the natural 20, Aniess also read three things:** Setha wears the clothing of a
founding family of Maalm, she is used to being obeyed, and she has contempt for the
Tally House and everyone in it.

## Calix is a woman, and Setha wants her

**Calix Vire is female.** `[settled 9/1]` The token was, so she is — corrected through
[the rival crews](../world/suartleheim-eet/maalm/encounters/tavern-rival-crews.md) and the
Aar files.

**Setha has a crush on Calix.** Aniess rolled high on who in the room Setha was most
interested in, and that is the answer. `[new — Will 9/1]` The Writ's leader wants her own
pathfinder, who takes her orders from a demon that belongs to Preem.

## Blackacre went upstairs

He left the room and searched the guest house.

- **Empty rooms**, and **locked doors**.
- **The bath house** — a big room, fires lit, a large tub, water being heated. This is
  Yessa drawing it for the ones sailing in the morning.
- **He turned into a mouse and got into Setha's room**, then came back out to the
  kitchen.

**So the party have seen the room the knife is kept in**, a night before they take it.

## Jasper

**He drank with Beartholomew and said little.**

## Where it stands

- **The theft has not happened.** The Writ's boat goes in the morning and the knife is
  still on Setha's belt.
- **The bath is heated.** Blackacre saw the fires lit. That is the pre-dawn draw, and the
  last chance at the belt after the gallery
  ([the run to Copaa](../world/suartleheim-eet/raand-copaa/encounters/the-run-to-copaa.md), Part Two).
- **Aniess knows what the statue is made of** and the party have not decided what that
  means for handing the knife to Ottmar Lisk at Copaa.
- **Setha knows Aniess works for Preem.** Two of Preem's crews have now identified each
  other by name.
- `[OPEN]` **Doruk's secret mission.** He volunteered that the Writ are going to Brauron
  for something other than the Hunt, and would not say what.
- `[OPEN]` **The 5,000 gp.** The party cannot buy in as hunters, so they will have to get into the
  Sacred Hunt some other way
  ([the game preserve](../world/suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/the-game-preserve.md)).

## Threads

- **Pax's mushrooms.** Nineteen dried and six fresh left. One dose, one hour, advantage on
  Insight, no cost yet.
- **Pim has one of them.** Pax handed a mushroom to a man who is already taking four
  compounds a day and cannot go down a hole sober.
- **The fight never started.** Doruk and Mogg were both drunk in the same room all evening
  and nobody told either of them what the other is.
