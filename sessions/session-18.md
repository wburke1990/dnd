# Session 18 — The Mounted Hunters

> Notes taken during play. Picks up from [session 17](session-17.md). The fight began in the
> sitting before and finished in this one.

The party fight five Maalm hunters on great hounds in
[the game preserve](../world/suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/the-game-preserve.md). Run sheet:
[the mounted hunters](../world/suartleheim-eet/brauron/encounters/the-mounted-hunters-run-sheet.md).

## The fight

- **Sarric casts *Animal Friendship* on all five hounds.** Four buck their riders and run to
  him to eat rat jerky. The fifth stays charmed with its rider still in the saddle.
- The four thrown riders go for Sarric on foot.
- The leader has a longbow (+3 to hit, 1d8 + 1).
- Three hunters are killed.
- The fourth surrenders.
- **Sarric knocks the leader out.** The leader is at 0 hit points,
  stable.

## The leader's house

The hunters belong to **House Varek**, a Maalm noble house. The leader is a son of the house. The noble houses came to Maalm with
Bedragon.

If the leader dies, House Varek puts a bounty on whoever killed him. The hunter who surrendered
will tell House Varek.

## After the fight

- **Sarric proposes killing both prisoners**, the leader and the hunter who surrendered.
- **Blackacre wants to leave the bodies for the swamp.**
- **Sarric agrees to let the leader live in exchange for information.** Then he leaves the
  leader tied up for the tigers.
- The fourth hunter surrendered to Aniess. Sarric disarms him and lets him go.
  Sarric tells him that if anyone asks what happened to the son of House Varek, he should say
  *"I don't know."*
- `[Will]` What the leader tells them.

## The hounds' names

- Sarric names his hound **Jubjub**.
- Blackacre names his **Don Junior**, then renames it **New Boat**.
- Jasper names his **Ivan**.
- Aniess and Pax name the other two hounds next week.

## On the bodies

Prepped loot:

- Five sets of scale mail, shields, lances and hunting swords. Hound-pelt cloaks.
  Hunting horns. The leader's longbow.
- Waterskins of clean water, about two days' worth for each rider. All the water inside the
  wall is poison.
- Dried meat and bread, rope, a net, skinning knives, a bone saw, a pot of salt.
- Heads of commoners tied to the saddles by their hair.
- A strip of the quarry's deer-hide. Whoever carries it smells like the quarry to the other
  hounds in the preserve.
- Coin purses with Maalm silver, 2d10 gp each. A signet ring with the Varek seal on each
  hunter.
- Five charmed great hounds, ridable at Speed 60, for the 24 hours the spell lasts.
