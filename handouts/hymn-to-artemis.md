# Homeric Hymn 27 — To Artemis

> **Handout — hymn.** Homeric Hymn 27, "To Artemis," trans. Hugh G. Evelyn-White
> (1914). The goddess of the hunt — golden shafts, the hounds, the shooter of stags.
> Kept verbatim. Source: Perseus Digital Library
> (http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0138:hymn=27).

```
I sing of Artemis, whose shafts are of gold, who cheers on the hounds,
the pure maiden, shooter of stags, who delights in archery, own sister
to Apollo with the golden sword. Over the shadowy hills and windy peaks
she draws her golden bow, rejoicing in the chase, and sends out grievous
shafts.
```
