# Myrrha and the Birth of Adonis — Metamorphoses X

> **Handout — epic.** Ovid, *Metamorphoses* X.298–518, trans. Arthur Golding
> (1567), the same translation as [Actaeon](actaeon.md). Myrrha, pregnant by
> her father and hunted for it, asks to be neither living nor dead; the gods
> turn her into the myrrh tree. She is bark to the neck and her eyes are gone,
> and she is still pregnant. The tree swells, bends, and groans, and the bark
> splits and the child Adonis comes out. The gum that runs down the
> trunk afterwards is her crying.
