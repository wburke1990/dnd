# Momotaro — the Son of a Peach

> **Handout — fairy tale.** "Momotaro, or the Story of the Son of a Peach," from
> *Japanese Fairy Tales* by Yei Theodora Ozaki (1908). A childless couple find a
> great peach in the river; when it is cut, a child steps out. Kept verbatim. Source:
> Lit2Go/USF (https://etc.usf.edu/lit2go/72/japanese-fairy-tales/) and Project
> Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4018).

```
As she was busy washing her clothes a great peach came bumping down the
stream.
```

```
He brought out the kitchen knife, and, placing the peach on a board, was
about to cut it when, wonderful to tell, the peach split in two of itself
and a clear voice said: "Wait a bit, old man!" and out stepped a
beautiful little child.
```
