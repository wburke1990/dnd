# The Birth of Attis

> **Handout — myth.** Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 7.17.11, trans. W. H. S.
> Jones (Loeb Classical Library, 1918–35). An almond-tree grows from a slain god's
> severed parts; a river-god's daughter takes the fruit to her bosom and conceives
> Attis. Kept verbatim. Source: theoi.com (https://www.theoi.com/Text/Pausanias7B.html).

```
There grew up from the severed genitals an almond-tree with its fruit
ripe, and a daughter of the river Sangarius took of the fruit and laid
it in her bosom, when it at once disappeared, but she was with child.
A boy was born, and exposed, but was tended by a he-goat.
```
