# The Juniper-Tree

> **Handout — fairy tale.** The Brothers Grimm, "The Juniper-Tree," from *Grimm's
> Household Tales*, trans. Margaret Hunt (1884). A murdered boy is buried beneath the
> juniper; his soul stays in the tree and rises as a bird that sings. Kept verbatim.
> Source: worldoftales.com / Wikisource. Note: Hunt's wording is "killed me"; the
> "slew me" version comes from other translations (Lucy Crane, Edgar Taylor).

The mother's death and burial:

```
…she was so delighted that she died. Then her husband buried her beneath
the juniper-tree…
```

The bird's song:

```
My mother she killed me,
My father he ate me,
My sister, little Marlinchen,
Gathered together all my bones,
Tied them in a silken handkerchief,
Laid them beneath the juniper-tree,
Kywitt, kywitt, what a beautiful bird am I!
```
