# Sayid's Writ

Found in the ship's mail at [the drifting wreck](../world/the-sea/encounters/the-drifting-wreck.md),
in nine copies, each addressed to a different harbour. Print it and hand it to Sam.

> **Fill in the name before the session.** Sam is confirming Blackacre's birth name.
> Until he does, leave the blank — it looks like space left for the name.

---

```
Put out by Sayid, who speaks for his tribes, at sea, this season.

There is a man wanted.

______________________, a dragonborn, a druid, of the Amatssou, out of
Tripoca. He sails alone in a lashed sloop, small, fast, of our build. He
goes under the name BLACKACRE, and he has gone under others.

He is wanted alive. Bring him to any hull of ours and you will be paid two
hundred in gold. We do not pay in timber.

If he will not be brought alive he is wanted anyway, and the price is half.

Say to him that the work is not finished and that it is still his, and that
he can come aboard on his own feet.

Say to him also that he was asked once.
```

---

## Notes

- **Nine copies, nine harbours** — Mukka, Aar, Aal, and six more. None of them was
  addressed to Blackacre.
- **"We do not pay in timber"** — the fleets cannot grow wood and cannot buy it in
  quantity ([floating fleet](../world/the-sea/lore/floating-fleet.md)). The price is two
  hundred in gold; timber is not offered.
- **The name in capitals** is the sea-name. Any dragonborn druid at sea recognises it as
  a sea-name ([the Wandering](../world/the-sea/lore/wandering-dragonborn.md)); a
  harbourmaster reads it as a name.
