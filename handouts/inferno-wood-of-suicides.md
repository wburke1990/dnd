# The Wood of the Suicides — Inferno XIII

> **Handout — epic.** Dante, *Inferno*, Canto XIII. A wood with no green in it,
> black leaves and knotted boughs. Virgil tells Dante to break off a twig.
> Dante does, and the trunk bleeds and cries out; the voice is Pier della
> Vigna, who says that they were men and are now stocks, and that a tree here
> makes no sound at all until it is torn. At the end of the world the bodies
> will be brought back and hung on the trees they used to be.
