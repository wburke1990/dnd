# Actaeon — the Stag Torn by His Hounds

> **Handout — myth.** Ovid, *Metamorphoses* Book 3, trans. Arthur Golding (1567) —
> *The .xv. Bookes of P. Ouidius Naso, entytuled Metamorphosis*. Diana turns the
> hunter Actaeon into a stag, and his own hounds tear him apart. Kept verbatim (in
> Golding's Elizabethan spelling). Source: Wikisource
> (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Metamorphoses_(tr._Golding)/Book_3).
>
> `[to confirm]` The Wikisource scan reads "homes" in the first line — an OCR error
> for Golding's **"hornes"**, restored here; confirm against a clean facsimile before
> final use.

The transformation:

```
A payre of lively olde Harts hornes upon his sprinckled head.
She sharpes his eares, she makes his necke both slender, long and lanke.
She turnes his fingers into feete, his armes to spindle shanke.
She wrappes him in a hairie hyde beset with speckled spottes,
```

The hounds:

```
They hem him in on everie side, and in the shape of Stagge,
With greedie teeth and griping pawes their Lord in peeces dragge.
```
